STATUS EFFECTS & BUFF STACKING

Other Systems › Status Effects & Buff Stacking

Every buff and debuff in the game belongs to exactly one ability. Two different abilities stack; one ability never stacks with itself. Magnitudes on the same stat combine additively, never multiplicatively, so a +50% and a +15% is +65% — not ×1.725.

Identity: a status is its ability

A status is identified by the ability that applied it, not by the stat it moves. Every buff payload a move produces is stamped at build time with:

  • key = 'ab:' + Move_ID — e.g. ab:M-STORMFATHER-4. This is the identity the engine matches on.
  • name = the ability's own stage name (Stormcall / Call the Storm / Convocation of Storms). This is what the player sees on the status chip.

The key is stamped onto every buff clause of the move — its self buff, its ally buff, its single-target ally buff and its Synergy clause — so all clauses of one ability share one identity. Riders, marks and DoTs applied to enemies keep their authored names (Mothervenom, Impale) and are identified by that name.

Stacking rules

SituationResult
Two different abilities buff the same statBoth statuses exist side by side. Their magnitudes add.
Two clauses of the same ability land on one unitOne status. Same key, so they merge — they do not stack.
The same ability is cast againDuration refreshes to the new cast. Magnitude keeps whichever is stronger. Never a second instance.

"Stronger" means further from the baseline in the direction the payload points: 1.0 for multipliers, 0 for flat adds. A +50% Attack buff re-applied at +35% stays +50%; a −40% Attack debuff re-applied at −25% stays −40%. The chip takes the name of the cast whose magnitude survived, so a weaker later cast cannot relabel a stronger running buff.

This is what caps buff spam. Stoke has Cooldown 1; without same-ability merging a player would reach +50%, +100%, +150% on consecutive turns.

Aggregation and clamps

Every status contributing to a stat has its delta summed once, then applied:

atkMult = clamp(1 + Σ(atkMul − 1) × stacks, 0.30, 2.50)

  • Attack and Defense follow the formula above.
  • Speed is a flat add: stages sum directly, no multiplier, no clamp beyond the stage system.
  • Damage taken follows the same additive rule and the same 0.30–2.50 clamp, summed over the target's statuses at hit time.
  • Because deltas sum, a buff and a debuff on one stat cancel point for point: +50% and −30% is ×1.20.

Legacy named statuses that carry no numeric payload contribute fixed deltas: Slagbite and Fracture −22% Defense, Gravechill −18% Attack and −2 Speed, Cinch −3 Speed, Doomrune +18% damage taken, Warded −18% damage taken.

Synergy clauses and the caster

A move's Synergy clause is a second buff clause gated on creature type. Two rules govern whether the caster is included:

  • A type-gated buff includes the caster when the caster is of that type. A Dragon casting "Dragon allies gain +15% Attack" is a Dragon and receives it. This is not a special case — the caster is an ally.
  • A clause that requires another ally of a type excludes the caster from that test. "If no Undead ally, self +30% Attack" asks whether some other party member is Undead; the caster does not satisfy its own requirement.

Because the Synergy buff carries the same ability key as the move's own self buff, a caster who qualifies for both gets one status at the stronger of the two magnitudes. A weak type synergy can never overwrite the ability's own stronger self buff.

Worked example — Convocation of Storms

M-STORMFATHER-4, Targets=self, Effect_S3 Self +50% Attack, 3t, Synergy Elemental and Dragon allies gain +15% Attack, 3t. Party: Riptide (Elemental·Dragon, the caster), Ignis (Elemental·Dragon, already running its own Feed the Conflagration at +50%), and a Construct character.

Targeting

The Targets column in moves.csv routes sides and is never inferred from Power. It governs selection as well as resolution:

  • Targets=self — only the caster is highlighted and only the caster can be clicked.
  • Support moves that reach one ally highlight the living allies.
  • Hostile moves highlight the living enemies.
  • An illegal click is refused. It is never silently redirected to a legal unit, which previously made a self-only buff look as though it could be aimed at a party member.

Changelog

  • 2026-07-28 — page created, and the buff system rebuilt beneath it. Previously every positive Attack buff was named Rallied and every positive Defense buff Guarded, and applying one filtered out any existing status of that name. Three consequences, all reported from live play: a move's own Synergy clause overwrote its self buff, so casting Convocation of Storms left the caster at +15% instead of +50%; one creature's buff overwrote another creature's unrelated buff; and the newer cast always won regardless of strength. Fixed by making a status's identity its ability. In the same pass: buffs now combine additively rather than multiplicatively; re-application refreshes duration and keeps the stronger magnitude; a Synergy type gate includes the caster while an "if no X ally" fallback excludes it; and Targets=self moves no longer offer allies as targets.
