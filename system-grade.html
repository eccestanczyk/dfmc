GRADE MULTIPLIER

Other Systems › Grade Multiplier

Every creature has a quality grade from Worn to Void. Grade applies one shared multiplier to a creature's stats — identical across every species — so grade is a clean, universal power axis independent of type or role.

The multiplier

Base stats come from axis grade + level. That base is then scaled by the quality multiplier:

QM = 0.84 + 0.04 × step   (Worn = step 0 … Void = step 9)

  • Applies to Attack, Defense and HP. Utility is an axis grade shown on the creature sheet; no multiplier touches it.
  • Never applies to Speed — Speed equals axis grade flat (1–10), with no quality, level, or gear scaling.
  • Grade is rolled at hatch, weighted by egg grade (up to 4 grades above the egg, any grade below — see the Eggs codex), and is the same for all species: a Void creature and a Worn creature of the same axis grades differ only by this multiplier.

Grade on gear

Items carry the same ten grades, and on a piece of gear the grade does two things:

  • It multiplies the piece’s stat. Every item effect is written N × T × GF — a base number, times the tier (I–X), times the grade factor GF: ×1.00 at Worn, ×1.48 at Epic, ×2.08 at Void. A Void piece pays a little over twice what the same piece pays at Worn.
  • It sets how many sockets the piece holds. A Worn piece has none; a Rare piece holds 1 gem, 1 rune and 1 scroll socket; a Void piece 3 / 3 / 3. Sockets follow the grade, never the tier — see Sockets.
  • Gear grade is raised at the upgrade bench, one grade at a time, with a success chance that falls as the grade rises (a failed attempt spends the cost and leaves the piece as it was) — the odds and costs are on Upgrading. A creature’s grade is rolled once at hatch and never changes.
  • Souls have their own grade multiplier, applied to the passive or move they lend; it is tabled under Soul strength on Equipment.
Grade is one of two independent gear/creature power axes. It differs from axis grade (which shapes a creature's stat distribution). Full numerical stat tables live in the Stat & Equipment Tables.
