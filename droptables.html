DROPTABLES

How Drops Work

Every wild creature defeated makes three independent rolls — one for gear, one for an enhancement (gem / rune / scroll, even split), and one for the defeated line's Stage-1 egg. Each roll uses the same per-floor chance table below. When a gear roll succeeds, the piece is an even pick among the gear bases of that floor's tier (weapons, armour, accessories, tags); a successful enhancement roll splits evenly across gem / rune / scroll, even within the family.

Per-kill chances are scaled on the game-wide rarity value ladder — C 1.0 / UC 1.5 / R 2.5 / SR 4.0 / SSR 7.0 — and balanced per floor against that floor's mix of encounters, so each drop family averages 0.2 drops per battle (3 kills; one drop every five battles) on every floor of the tower. Rarer kills pay better; the floor-wide economy never drifts.

The tables on this page are read live from the same data the game uses.

Drop Grade Weights by Floor Band

When a gear drop lands, its grade is rolled from the band of the floor it dropped on. Divine, Primordial, and Void never drop — grading gear (paid in Scrap) is the only path to Divine+ gear, and breeding the only path to Divine+ creatures.

Band shifts: Enhancements (gems, runes, scrolls) roll their grade one band behind gear. Boss gear rolls one band ahead of the boss's band (the band after the tower’s 91–100 row is the first Void Apex row, so the floor-100 boss rolls floor 101’s Rare-and-up row; Apex rows are one floor wide, so an Apex boss rolls one floor ahead (floor 102 reads 103) and floor 120 clamps to its own row). Wild and boss eggs use the floor band clamped to the Legendary wild-egg cap.

Floors 101–120 use their own grade rows. The Void Apex postgame zone has one single-floor band per Apex floor, with four grades only — Rare, Epic, Legendary, Abyssal — so Rare is the floor and Divine, Primordial and Void never drop. Eggs use the same curve with the Abyssal share folded into Legendary. Enhancements still roll one floor behind, except on floor 101, which reads its own row. Apex gear drops at tier 10.

Boss Keys

A single zone-specific Key is needed to challenge a boss, and is spent when the fight starts; walking into the den is free. Every wild creature defeated on the zone's 9 preceding floors rolls for that zone's Key, scaled on the rarity value ladder, at most one Key per battle. With the gate below open, that is about one Key every 15 battles in Grasslands, rising to about one every 90 battles in Flaming Hellscape.

Guaranteed key: the first time a character clears the 9th floor of a tower zone (floors 9, 19, … 99), a Key is guaranteed to drop — once per zone per character. Void Apex keys are a pure roll, with no guaranteed key.

LOADING…

Gates: a zone's Key cannot drop until the character has banked that zone's Min Wins wins AND Min Kills wild kills, counted per zone, wild battles only — a boss win advances neither. Below either gate the roll is skipped entirely.

A Key that drops into a full bag is LOST. Every drop needs a free bag slot: with none, the drop is discarded, never queued and never held — the victory screen reports only "N drops lost — inventory full" and does not say a Key was among them. Keys are the rarest item in a zone (0.46% per Common kill in Desert Plains, about one per 40 battles), so keep a slot free while you farm them.

Void Apex rate: on each Apex key floor (101, 103, … 119) one Key is expected every two hours at one won battle every 15 seconds — 1,440 kills per Key, split by the rarity ladder.

Boss Loot

Every boss kill guarantees two drops, delivered on the battle-conclusion screen straight to inventory:

One piece of equipment — an even pick among the Weapon / Armour / Accessory bases, at the boss floor's tier, with grade rolled one band ahead of the boss's floor band: the band after the tower’s 91–100 row is the first Void Apex row, so the floor-100 boss rolls floor 101’s Rare-and-up row; Apex rows are one floor wide, so an Apex boss rolls one floor ahead (floor 102 reads 103) and floor 120 clamps to its own row. Divine+ never drops.

One native egg — a Stage-1 egg of any line that spawns on the zone’s floors (a line counts when any of its stages spawns there; the egg is its Stage 1) — 69–87 lines per tower zone, not just the zone’s ten home lines, weighted by the rarity chances 30/30/20/10/10 (C/UC/R/SR/SSR) spread over the tiers present in the zone. Grade from the boss's floor band, clamped to the Legendary wild-egg cap — breeding remains the only path to Divine+ and Void creatures. Void Apex bosses (102–120): the pool is every line in the game, and each Apex boss floor sets its own tier weights instead of the 30/30/20/10/10 above; the egg can be any line, including the lines of the three creatures in the fight. Full mechanics on Void Apex.

First-Win Bonus Drops

The first 10 wild-battle wins each grant an additional guaranteed drop on top of the normal rolls. All grants spawn at Worn, Tier 1. Boss wins do not advance the counter. Win 1 rolls the accessory family at random; win 4 grants the complement. Egg grants (wins 1 and 9) give the Stage-1 egg of the lowest-rarity enemy present, random among ties.
