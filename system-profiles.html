PROFILES, BADGES & ACHIEVEMENTS

Hub › Other Systems › Profiles, Badges & Achievements

Every player has a public profile, and every profile carries the badges the account has earned. Badges are awarded by the server when the fact becomes true in your save; nothing on your device can award one. On Steam the same facts also unlock Steam achievements.

The profile

  • Open it from the rankings — click a name on any leaderboard (PvP, timers & leaderboards) and the profile opens over the board.
  • What it shows — the avatar (your Steam avatar when you signed in through Steam), the character's name and class, the active party, the highest floor, the PvP rating, the team's power, and the badge row.
  • After a name change — the card shows “formerly” and the old name for 30 days, while the old name is held.
  • Read-only and public — anyone on the board can see it. There is no profile text to edit.

Invite a friend

Your invite code and link are in Settings → Account. A friend who starts a new climb with it, reaches floor 30 and keeps the account for a week earns you both 100 Wraithgold, for up to ten friends. Sharing the link by itself gives nothing.

Badges

Each badge is awarded once, when the server sees the fact in your save; a badge the server has not awarded does not exist, whatever the device thinks. A Reset Progress wipes them with the rest of the climb.

Steam achievements

Player level

The account-wide player level on the profile is cosmetic and uncapped; it is a separate ladder from creature, class and character level and is described on XP & Leveling.
