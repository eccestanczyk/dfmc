PROFILES, BADGES & ACHIEVEMENTS

Hub › Other Systems › Profiles, Badges & Achievements

Every player has a public profile, and every profile carries the badges the account has earned. Badges are the server's: it awards them when the fact becomes true in a save it accepted, and nothing on the device can mint one. On Steam the same facts also unlock Steam achievements.

The profile

  • Open it from the rankings — click a name on any leaderboard (PvP, timers & leaderboards) and the profile opens over the board.
  • What it shows — the avatar (your Steam avatar when you signed in through Steam), the character's name and class, the active party, the highest floor, the PvP rating, the team's power, and the badge row.
  • Read-only and public — anyone on the board can see it; it writes nothing and mints nothing. There is no profile text to edit.

On the Steam build the same server-side facts unlock achievements (codex/achievements.csv, pushed through /api/steam/achievements). Most are badges under another name; three are not badges at all. Hidden ones do not show their description on Steam until earned.

Player level

The account-wide player level on the profile is cosmetic and uncapped; it is a separate ladder from creature, class and character level and is described on XP & Leveling.

Implementation notes

  • Profile read: GET /api/profile/<name> (B15) — a WORLD path, 0 writes. Client profileOpen(name) fills state.profileView (profName, profCls, profParty, profFloor, profRate, profPower, profBadges).
  • Badge rows and their holder counters live in the world object (badges, badge_counts) and go with the boards on a wipe or a T61 reset. /api/_internal/badges_of is the ops read.
  • Avatars: the Steam avatar URL is checked by STEAM_AV_OK before it is drawn.
