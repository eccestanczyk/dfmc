† DEVOUR

Other Systems › Devour

One creature consumes another — or a Devour item — and takes what was worth keeping. The fodder's XP always transfers in full. Grade and mutant status transfer too, but only between the same species. The fodder is destroyed permanently: its uid is burned and nothing recovers it.

Where it lives

  • Soul bag: open a creature's sheet — † DEVOUR sits beside DISMANTLE. That creature is the consumer.
  • Equipment window: click an equipped creature's slot — † DEVOUR sits under ATTEMPT UPGRADE. The equipped creature is the consumer, and its gains (level, grade → QM, mutant) re-derive the party immediately.
  • The panel shows the consumer on the left with the arrow pointing at it, and the fodder slot on the right. Clicking the slot opens the picker.
  • Devour only opens on the floor or in the boss room. During a battle (select through flash) it refuses with NOT DURING A BATTLE. While the panel is open the floor simulation is frozen, like every other window.

What can be consumed

The fodder picker lists the soul bag's visible pool only: creatures and Devour consumables. By construction this means anything equipped, penned or socketed can never be fodder — those are not in the visible pool. Eggs and class souls are never fodder. The consumer is excluded from its own pool; a creature cannot devour itself. Consumables sort to the top of the picker, then creatures in soul-bag order.

Devouring a creature

AspectRule
XPAlways transfers, in full, regardless of species: consumer.xp += fodder.xp. Level is re-derived from the shared XP curve.
GradeTransfers only between the same species, and only upward: the consumer keeps the higher of the two grades. Grade feeds the Grade Multiplier, so this is a real stat change.
MutantTransfers only between the same species: a mutant fodder makes the consumer a mutant (see Mutant Variants). Never removed, never reversed.
FodderDestroyed. The record leaves the bag, the uid is burned, equipment is unaffected (a bag creature carries none).
IdentityThe consumer's uid, origin, gender and everything not listed above are untouched. It is the same creature, fed.

“Same species” means the same creature, not the same line. Species identity is the creature's codex row (its DFMC image id, falling back to name). An S1 and the S3 it advances into are different species for this rule — devouring one into the other transfers XP only.

The Devour consumables

Three item types live in the soul bag under the new Consumable category. None of them can be obtained anywhere yet (D ruling) — no drop, no craft, no purchase mints them; mkDevourItem is the single mint and nothing calls it in play. A refused consumable is never spent.

Blood PelletGrants XP to the consumer. Currently 1,000 XP — a placeholder constant (DEVOUR_PELLET_XP) pending tuning; each pellet record can also carry its own xp.None.

Order of operations

  • One judgement function (dvJudge) computes the outcome for both the preview line and the execution, so what the panel promises is exactly what commits.
  • The commit is one atomic state update: fodder removed (or its stack decremented) and consumer rewritten in the same write. If the world moved underneath — the fodder left the bag, the slot changed occupants — the updater bails and nothing is paid.
  • The consumer is resolved live on every read: a bag consumer must still be in the bag, a slot consumer is whatever the slot holds now.
  • Each successful devour writes a DEVOUR adventure-log event and saves the run. The panel stays open so the player can keep feeding the same creature.

Edge cases and consequences

  • Advancement is not triggered from the panel. Devoured XP can carry a creature past its advancement level, but the advancement scene plays where it always does: a party creature advances on its next victory; a bag creature advances once it is equipped and wins. The XP is banked either way.
  • A grade raise on an equipped consumer changes its quality multiplier at once — the party re-derives and the power number moves.
  • Devour only ever removes from the bag, so the inventory guard is not in play — no path here returns an item.
  • Stacked consumables decrement quantity; the last one leaves the bag.

Integrity

Devour resolves entirely client-side, like crafting and grading: the result rides the save, and the server does not yet validate creature XP, grade or the mutant flag. It therefore belongs to the same anticheat class as those systems — the Phase 2b transaction gate (server-rolled outcomes) covers it when it lands. Until the items are obtainable, nothing of economic value is minted here: a devour can only concentrate what the account already legitimately holds into fewer creatures.
