† DEVOUR

Other Systems › Devour

One creature consumes another — or a Devour item — and takes what was worth keeping. The fodder's XP always transfers in full. Grade and mutant status transfer too, but only within the same line. The fodder is destroyed permanently: nothing recovers it. Devour opens on floor 12 (Unlocks).

Where it lives

  • Soul bag: open a creature's sheet — † DEVOUR sits beside DISMANTLE. That creature is the consumer.
  • Equipment window: click an equipped creature's slot — † DEVOUR sits under ATTEMPT UPGRADE. The equipped creature is the consumer, and its gains (level, grade → quality multiplier, mutant) apply to the party at once.
  • Soul-socket picker: open a gear slot's soul picker while the socket holds a creature — † DEVOUR is offered there too. The socketed creature is the consumer, so a socketed soul can be levelled without unsocketing it. An empty socket or a class soul has no creature, and the button is not shown.
  • The panel shows the consumer on the left with the arrow pointing at it, and the fodder slot on the right. Clicking the slot opens the fodder picker; only that list scrolls.
  • Devour only opens on the floor or in the boss room. During a battle it refuses with NOT DURING A BATTLE. While the panel is open the floor is paused, like every other window.

The fodder picker

  • It lists soul-bag creatures and Devour consumables. Eggs and class souls are never fodder, a locked creature is never offered, and a creature cannot devour itself.
  • It offers only fodder that can change the consumer — more XP, a better grade or mutant status. The preview line and the result use the same judgement, so what the panel promises is what you get.
  • Creatures that are equipped (a party slot or a soul socket) or breeding (in a pen) are shown greyed and badged EQUIPPED or BREEDING, after the selectable ones. They cannot be picked; clicking one says why.
  • If nothing selectable is left, the picker says so.

This is a different order from the party creature picker on Equipment: there you choose a creature to fight with, here one to spend, and the line decides whether grade and mutant transfer.

Devouring a creature

AspectRule
XPAlways transfers, in full, regardless of species. Level is re-derived from the shared XP curve.
GradeTransfers within the same LINE, and only upward: the consumer keeps the higher of the two grades. Any stage feeds any other stage of its own line. Grade feeds the Grade Multiplier, so this is a real stat change.
MutantTransfers within the same LINE, across stages: a mutant fodder makes the consumer a mutant (see Mutant Variants). Never removed, never reversed.
FodderDestroyed. A bag creature carries no equipment, so nothing else is lost.
IdentityThe consumer's gender, origin and everything not listed above are untouched. It is the same creature, fed.

“Same” means the same LINE. A Stage-1 creature and the Stage-3 it advances into are the same line: feed a Stage-1 Rare mutant into its own Stage-3 Common and the Stage-3 comes out Rare and mutant. Across different lines only XP moves.

  • The result comes from the server, judged from your saved creatures; a reload cannot change it. A devour that would change nothing is refused.
  • If a devour is refused or the connection drops, nothing is consumed and the panel says so. Retrying the same fodder gives the same answer.
  • Each devour is saved at once. The panel stays open so you can keep feeding the same creature.

The Devour consumables

Three item types live in the soul bag under the Consumable category. No drop, craft or purchase gives them in the current game. A refused consumable is never spent; stacked consumables are used one at a time.

After a devour

  • Advancement does not happen in the panel. Devoured XP can carry a creature past its advancement level; the advancement scene plays where it always does: a party creature advances on its next victory, a bag creature once it is equipped and wins. The XP is banked either way.
  • A grade raise on an equipped consumer changes its quality multiplier at once — the party’s stats and your POWER move.
  • A devour never adds anything to the bag, so a full bag never blocks it.
