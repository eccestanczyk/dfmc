The authored respawner gives one spawn per 60 seconds while below the cap of ten. Incense halves that interval to 30 seconds. The pool cap is unchanged — the cap is how many creatures can be waiting, not how fast they arrive.

How a purchase works

The client names a SKU and an idempotency key. It never sends a price. The worker resolves the authored row and the player's own object performs the balance check, the debit and the grant inside a single transaction. A repeated key returns the original answer rather than charging twice.

Insufficient balanceRefused with 402. A balance never goes negative on a purchase.

ExpiryComputed server-side from the grant. A save rollback can neither lose nor extend it.

The wallet ledger

Wraithgold is held in an append-only ledger. The balance is the sum of its entries; there is no authoritative balance stored anywhere else. Every entry carries its provenance, so paid, granted and promotional units stay distinguishable — that distinction cannot be reconstructed once units mix.

A progress wipe does not touch it. Resetting a realm clears saves and progress; currency that was paid for survives.

Item grants

Items owed to a player — compensation, purchases, promotions — are minted server-side and stay pending until the client takes delivery and records the grant in its own save. If a save is rolled back, the record rolls back with it and the grant becomes owed again, so a restore can never silently swallow an item. A grant with nowhere to go waits rather than being destroyed.

The incense prompt (P7, shipped 2026-08-03)

When a floor's spawns are exhausted and no boon is running, the floor offers BURN INCENSE TO GET MORE SPAWNS. incPromptOn and onIncensePrompt had been computed and inert in app.js with no markup at all — the block was removed and never rebuilt. Six candidate causes were ruled out across earlier attempts (the scene, the condition, a missing spawnSb, wiped markup, a missing hint-placeholder-val, and placement) without finding one, because the block was not in the document to begin with. It is deliberately a sibling of the drought gate rather than nested inside it: nesting in the drought pill is the placement that was already tried and emitted nothing.

It shows only when the pool is empty and no Incense boon is active, and hides the moment one is burned. Probe: tools/probe_deadprops.py (8/8), which asserts the condition is true before believing the render.

Incense — the player can see it burning

Incense doubles wild-spawn refill for 30 days. The expiry has always been server-side (spawn_budget.boon_until) and /api/floor/enter has always returned it to the client as inc, which the client stored as spawnInc — and nothing ever rendered it, so a player who had paid for Incense had no way to tell how much was left. The floor screen now shows a pill reading INCENSE · 4d 23h, under the MENU button. Coarse by design: the boon runs 30 days, so days and hours are the useful unit.

Placement was measured, not chosen. The obvious spot — centred under the floor title — is covered by the floor header bar, which is z-index 80 with an opaque background. A pill placed there is present in the DOM with every computed style reading visible, and invisible on screen. The centre lower down belongs to the incense prompt (top:12%), which can be showing at the same time. Under MENU is clear, and z-index 105 clears the bar. Verify this class of change with document.elementFromPoint() at the element's own centre, never by reading its text.

Panel size

The shop was the only panel in the game not on the full-bleed pattern — a fixed 520px column while inventory, equipment, breeding and settings are all width:100%. It is now min(1180px,94%) at 86% height, the offers lay out in a responsive grid (auto-fill, minmax(360px,1fr)) rather than a single column, and the note and CLOSE are pinned to the bottom so a short catalogue does not leave the footer floating in the middle of an empty panel.
