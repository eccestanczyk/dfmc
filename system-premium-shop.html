Classes and skins

The CLASSES tab shows the classes you do not own yet, 500 Wraithgold each; they are still sold for Scrap or Ink in the CLASSES window (Classes).

Its SKINS section sells a Mutant skin for each stage-3 class, 400 Wraithgold, or all six for 2040. Already own some? The set costs only the skins you are missing, still 15% off. A skin bought early waits as a preview and goes on at stage 3. Skins are yours for good.

How Incense and Bait work

Cleared wild creatures come back to your floor one at a time. Up to ten can be waiting; the boons change how fast they return, not how many can wait.

RunningOne creature returns every
Nothing60 seconds
Incense30 seconds
Bait30 seconds
Incense and Bait15 seconds

A boon starts the moment you buy it. Time you had already waited counts at the old speed.

On the floor

  • The boon tiles. Four tiles sit under the MENU button on every floor: the Incense Burner, the Bait, Turbo Pens and the Lucky Charm. A running boon shows its icon and the time left (12d 4h); one that is not running is dimmed and reads NOT APPLIED. Hover a tile (or press and hold it on touch) to read what it does and how long is left; click or tap it to open the shop.
  • When the floor runs dry. With every creature cleared, the floor shows SPAWNS EXHAUSTED and a countdown to the next one. With no Incense burning it also offers BURN INCENSE TO GET MORE SPAWNS; with Incense burning and no Bait, ADD BAIT TO HALVE THE WAIT. Either one opens the shop.
  • The first dry floors. The first time a floor runs dry, an arrow points at the Incense tile: your first Incense is free (First Incense). Ten minutes after that lesson ends, the next dry floor points at the Bait tile (First Bait, also free).

The shop screen

The shop fills the screen: the Shadow Trader stands on the left, the offers sit on the right, and your Wraithgold is at the top. The cross in the top corner closes it. The SHADOW TRADER tab next to PREMIUM opens at floor 40 (Shadow Trader). Touch a Wraithgold coin on a price with the cursor’s flame and it catches fire; it keeps burning until you close the shop.

Wraithgold

  • Wraithgold is the premium currency. No account starts with any.
  • Besides the boons above it buys extra bag and soul-bag slots and breeding pens 4 to 6 (Breeding).
  • No random roll in the game is paid in Wraithgold: grade, soul-grade and every other roll costs currency you farm.
  • Reset Progress clears Wraithgold you earned or were given, and ends every running boon, with the rest of the climb. Wraithgold you paid real money for is kept (Accounts).
