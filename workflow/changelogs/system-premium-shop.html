Page intro and the sale table (SKU ids)

Server-authoritative store for Wraithgold. Prices and durations are authored in codex/premium_shop.csv — never in code.

The drought lessons and the one-time boons (#1171, 2026-11-03). The first time a floor runs dry (every creature cleared, the respawn budget short of the pool) an arrow points at the INCENSE floor tile (opening.csv drought_incense); the shop then offers First Incense, a 1-day Incense for 1 Scrap. Ten minutes after that lesson ends, the next dry floor points the arrow at the BAIT tile (drought_bait) and First Bait, a 1-day Bait for 1 Scrap. A premium_shop.csv row with Once = 1 is bought once per account, ever: the server keeps the purchase on the account, leaves the row out of that account’s catalogue and refuses a second purchase (409 once_bought). A row priced in a material is paid the Shadow Trader’s way: checked against the ledger (or the stored save before the wipe), booked as a spend, written into the stored save with a version bump, and the client adopts the new balance.

The authored respawner gives one spawn per 60 seconds while below the cap of ten. Incense halves that interval to 30 seconds. Bait multiplies whatever interval is running by 0.5: 30 seconds alone, 15 seconds with Incense (interval = (60 s − incense 30 s) × bait 0.5, tables.json spawn_budget). A timed boon bought again adds 30 days. Auto PVP Attacks is sold by the same door; the money is spent in the player's object and the ten tickets are granted in the world object on the purchase's own idem, so a retry ends at exactly +10 once. The pool cap is unchanged — the cap is how many creatures can be waiting, not how fast they arrive.

The boon starts when it is paid for and never reaches backwards. Credits accrue lazily — the row holds a balance and the instant it was last touched, and the accrual splits that window at the boon boundary, so time before the purchase settles at 60 seconds and time after it at 30. A purchase therefore settles the row first: it banks what the old rate earned, stamps the instant, and only then hangs the new expiry.

Until 2026-08-07 the purchase wrote the expiry alone and left the instant at the last spawn spent, so the whole already-elapsed window was re-accrued at the boosted rate — buying after three idle minutes handed the player six spawns for three minutes of ordinary waiting. Bounded by the cap of ten, and always in the player's favour, but paid time that was never paid for.

How a purchase works

The client names a SKU and an idempotency key. It never sends a price. The worker resolves the authored row and the player's own object performs the balance check, the debit and the grant inside a single transaction. A repeated key returns the original answer rather than charging twice.

RuleBehaviour
Insufficient balanceRefused with 402. A balance never goes negative on a purchase.
Repeat purchase while activeExtends the expiry by another 30 days. Time already paid for is never burned.
Unauthored SKURefused. Only rows in the CSV are for sale.
Catalogue unreachableRefuses to sell rather than inventing a price.
ExpiryComputed server-side from the grant. A save rollback can neither lose nor extend it.

The wallet ledger

Wraithgold is held in an append-only ledger. The balance is the sum of its entries; there is no authoritative balance stored anywhere else. Every entry carries its provenance, so paid, granted and promotional units stay distinguishable — that distinction cannot be reconstructed once units mix.

Paid Wraithgold survives a reset. A per-account reset (Settings; T61, D 2026-11-02: “per account reset should RESET everything”) clears the save and every ledger row that was earned or granted, and keeps every row that was paid for (paid_*). The full wipe of 2026-10 (T37) was the exception by D’s order: it took everything, entitlements included.

Item grants

Items owed to a player — compensation, purchases, promotions — are minted server-side and stay pending until the client takes delivery and records the grant in its own save. If a save is rolled back, the record rolls back with it and the grant becomes owed again, so a restore can never silently swallow an item. A grant with nowhere to go waits rather than being destroyed.

Paid taint (D11, 2026-11-03). Anything a purchase with Wraithgold or real money mints — a creature, an egg, a piece — is stamped paid on the server at the grant, and the stamp is never cleared: a paid egg hatches a paid creature, a creature that devours a paid one becomes paid, an evolve keeps it; breeding does not pass it (the egg costs farmed Ichor). A paid row can never go to the Steam market (system-integrity, “The Steam market mirror”). Today no SKU mints a row — the shop sells boons and PvP tickets and the packs sell Wraithgold only — so the stamp waits for the first M1 item that does.

Provenance and real-money Wraithgold (D5, built 2026-10-23, OFF)

Every ledger entry carries exactly one provenance class: earned, spent, refund, granted, promo, legacy, transfer, paid_web, paid_steam, paid_ios, paid_android. The server refuses any other value. Wraithgold is the only currency a store may sell.

No purchasable currency feeds a roll. A grade, soul grade or other roll step priced in Wraithgold is refused before the draw (403 paid_currency_roll), and the commit that would author one fails its gate. Rolls stay paid in farmed currency only, so no random draw is a paid chance.

Steam purchase, when switched on: the server opens the order, Steam shows its overlay, and only after Steam confirms the charge does the server add the pack's Wraithgold, once per order (paid_steam). Dismissal

The panel closes three ways: the CLOSE control, and — since 2026-08-05 — a tap anywhere on the backdrop. The backdrop carries onCloseShop and the window itself carries onStopProp, so a tap inside never bubbles out and closes it. This is the same pattern the shop backdrop simply carried no handler at all, so a tap outside did nothing. Verified with document.elementFromPoint() at a point derived from the backdrop's rect minus the window's — note that on a phone the stage is letterboxed, so the top-left of the SCREEN is page background and is not outside the window.
