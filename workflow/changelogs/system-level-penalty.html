Who gets XP

  • XP is awarded to the avatar’s class and both equipped creatures — the party is the three who fight. A creature swapped out mid-fight is paid in the bag; the account’s own level takes the full, unpenalised battle XP.
  • Dying in battle does not forfeit XP. A fainted creature or a downed avatar still receives its full end-of-battle share.
  • The penalty is computed per recipient, from that recipient's own level versus the defeated enemy's level. A low-level benchwarmer carried through a high-level fight keeps almost all of its XP; an over-leveled veteran farming weak floors keeps very little.

Where the numbers live

The curve’s five parameters (grace +10, full +50, grace −3, full −25, 90% cap) are one table, xp_penalty in dfmc-client src/tables.json, read by the server when it credits class XP for a battle (the server owns XP since the 2026-10-22 server-authority run) and mirrored by the game client’s victory screen. The per-kill base XP comes from XPCurve.csv — see XP.
