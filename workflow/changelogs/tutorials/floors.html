FLOORS AND THE FLOOR MAP — TEAM NOTES

The team record for the public page tutorials/floors.html: its dated changelog (newest first) and team notes. Lanes add each change here, never on the public page. Shape: python tools/codex_page.py --changelog tutorials/floors.html in dfmc-client.

Changelog

  • 2026-11-09 — the player walks the floor: to a creature before the fight, into a portal before the trip (ui#1224, after its first sentence; dfmc-client bug run chunk flooravatar). On dev. D 2026-11-08: the player character should appear on the floor, walk to the creature it challenges and engage there, stand where it engaged after the fight, and walk into a portal, be sucked in (distorted, spinning, smaller, into the centre) and come out the reverse way in the next room. Before, the player was not on the floor: a creature click opened the engage and a portal click started the trip at once. Now the equipped class art (no new art) stands on the floor with the creatures’ breath / walk cycle (gaitCls / gaitTick); a creature click walks it beside that creature, facing it, then fires the same engage; a portal click walks it to the portal’s centre, it is drawn in (favIn: a 720° spin, a side-to-side shear, scale to nothing, .55 s) and the same trip fires; the next room spits it out of the portal it arrived by (favOut, the reverse). After a fight it stands where it engaged. The walk is presentation only: one CSS transform transition capped at .8 s, a second click lands it at once, and the call that reaches the server is the one the click made before, unchanged. Transform and opacity only, no filter. Autoplay walks the avatar inside its existing pan cue (no added time). Low VFX / Reduce Motion / Battery saver / prefers-reduced-motion: no walk and no spin, the click acts at once. Probe tools/probe_flooravatar.py 2/10 on the old tree, 10/10 after. At release: on tutorials/floors.html step 1, after “Fight the creatures roaming the floor (1).” add “Your character walks over to the one you pick before the fight, and walks into a portal to take it.”
  • 2026-11-09 — the floor creatures breathe when still and walk when they roam (ui#1224, first sentence; dfmc-client bug run chunk flooranim). On dev. D 2026-11-08: when idle they should breathe, when moving they should walk, “same techniques we use on herumon dungeon”. Before, a floor creature only slid across the map with a slow 4.5 px hover bob, still or moving alike. Now each creature’s sprite sits in one wrapper (.fmob) that runs the Dungeon’s actor technique (its hud-idle-s / hud-gait) as CSS keyframes: fmBreath (3.4 s, a 1.4 % chest rise from the feet) while the roam sim holds it, fmWalk (.62 s, two steps: a 2.6 % bounce, a 1.4° lean each way, a squash on the plant) while it moves. Transform only, anchored at the feet; each creature’s phase is its own negative delay. The roam sim keeps the state: gaitTick reads the actor’s speed with hysteresis and swaps one class on a change, no per-frame style write; it takes any {vx,vy} actor and wrapper, so the floor avatar (the report’s later sentences, a later chunk) reuses it. Low VFX / Reduce Motion / Battery saver / prefers-reduced-motion: fm-still, the sprite holds still. Probe tools/probe_flooranim.py 1/7 on the old tree, 7/7 after. At release: no player-page edit (the page already says the creatures roam the floor; this is how they look doing it).
