FLOORS AND THE FLOOR MAP — TEAM NOTES

The team record for the public page tutorials/floors.html: its dated changelog (newest first) and team notes. Lanes add each change here, never on the public page. Shape: python tools/codex_page.py --changelog tutorials/floors.html in dfmc-client.

Changelog

  • 2026-11-10 — the character's walk no longer snaps back or teleports (ui#1253; dfmc-client bug run chunk avatarwalk). SHIPPED IN 0.99. Report (0.98): “the walking animation for the player looks very chooppy, and it often teleports around or snaps back weirdly”. Measured per frame (the drawn transform of [data-floor-avatar] under the game's re-renders): a plain walk was already continuous; every redirect was not. favEl rendered the avatar's transform from this.fav - where the walk began, or the turn point favStop wrote on a redirect - while favGo had set the walk target straight on the node. The next re-render after a redirect wrote the turn point back over the walk, so the avatar walked BACK to where it had turned and then jumped 450-800 stage px to the target when the walk timer ended. favEl now renders the walk's target while a walk runs (the spot the transition heads to), so a re-render never fights it. favStop reads the drawn spot off the computed transform (the time estimate stays as the fallback). An autoplay engage cue during the player's own creature or portal walk landed that walk at once (a teleport and an instant engage); the cue now leaves it alone and autoplay does not queue its own engage while the player's walk runs. Presentation only: nothing new reaches the server. Probe tools/probe_avatarwalk.py 3/7 on the old tree, 7/7 after; probe_floorwalk 8/8 and probe_flooravatar 10/10 unchanged. At release: none - the player page already says the character walks there.
  • 2026-11-10 — click the floor to walk there; the character walks, never dashes; the creatures step gentler (other#1246; dfmc-client bug run chunk floorwalk). SHIPPED IN 0.98. D 2026-11-10: “clicking around the map should move the character to that spot, even if no enemy was targeted. Monster walking animations are too frenetic, need to be gentler. When targeting an enemy the player character pretty much teleports over to its target, it needs to walk over.” Before, only a creature or portal click moved the avatar, and every walk was squeezed into at most .8 s (a dash across the map). Now a click on empty floor (favFloorWalk, the plate’s click) walks the avatar to that spot, clamped to the walkable band (x 3–97 %, y 14–88 %), and fires nothing. Every walk (floor, creature, portal) goes at one walking pace, 2.2 ms per stage px (about 450 px a second on the 1920×1080 stage, at most 3.2 s; FAV_MSPX / FAV_CAP), on a linear transition; the challenged creature holds still for the whole walk. A new floor click mid-walk redirects from where the avatar is (favStop); a creature or portal click during a floor walk walks on to it; a creature or portal click during a creature or portal walk still lands it at once, as before. Autoplay keeps its .6 s walk inside the pan cue. The roamers’ fmWalk step goes from .62 s with a 2.6 % bounce and 1.4° lean to 1.15 s, 1.6 % and .8°; the avatar, walking faster, keeps a .72 s step. Presentation only: nothing new reaches the server. Low VFX / Reduce Motion: no walk, a floor click places the avatar at once. Probe tools/probe_floorwalk.py 2/8 on the old tree, 8/8 after; tools/probe_flooravatar.py B3/B6 re-aimed to the walking pace (10/10). At release: on tutorials/floors.html step 1, after “Fight the creatures roaming the floor (1).” add “Click anywhere on the floor and your character walks there.”
  • 2026-11-09 — the player walks the floor: to a creature before the fight, into a portal before the trip (ui#1224, after its first sentence; dfmc-client bug run chunk flooravatar). SHIPPED IN 0.97. D 2026-11-08: the player character should appear on the floor, walk to the creature it challenges and engage there, stand where it engaged after the fight, and walk into a portal, be sucked in (distorted, spinning, smaller, into the centre) and come out the reverse way in the next room. Before, the player was not on the floor: a creature click opened the engage and a portal click started the trip at once. Now the equipped class art (no new art) stands on the floor with the creatures’ breath / walk cycle (gaitCls / gaitTick); a creature click walks it beside that creature, facing it, then fires the same engage; a portal click walks it to the portal’s centre, it is drawn in (favIn: a 720° spin, a side-to-side shear, scale to nothing, .55 s) and the same trip fires; the next room spits it out of the portal it arrived by (favOut, the reverse). After a fight it stands where it engaged. The walk is presentation only: one CSS transform transition capped at .8 s, a second click lands it at once, and the call that reaches the server is the one the click made before, unchanged. Transform and opacity only, no filter. Autoplay walks the avatar inside its existing pan cue (no added time). Low VFX / Reduce Motion / Battery saver / prefers-reduced-motion: no walk and no spin, the click acts at once. Probe tools/probe_flooravatar.py 2/10 on the old tree, 10/10 after. At release: on tutorials/floors.html step 1, after “Fight the creatures roaming the floor (1).” add “Your character walks over to the one you pick before the fight, and walks into a portal to take it.”
  • 2026-11-09 — the floor creatures breathe when still and walk when they roam (ui#1224, first sentence; dfmc-client bug run chunk flooranim). SHIPPED IN 0.97. D 2026-11-08: when idle they should breathe, when moving they should walk, “same techniques we use on herumon dungeon”. Before, a floor creature only slid across the map with a slow 4.5 px hover bob, still or moving alike. Now each creature’s sprite sits in one wrapper (.fmob) that runs the Dungeon’s actor technique (its hud-idle-s / hud-gait) as CSS keyframes: fmBreath (3.4 s, a 1.4 % chest rise from the feet) while the roam sim holds it, fmWalk (.62 s, two steps: a 2.6 % bounce, a 1.4° lean each way, a squash on the plant) while it moves. Transform only, anchored at the feet; each creature’s phase is its own negative delay. The roam sim keeps the state: gaitTick reads the actor’s speed with hysteresis and swaps one class on a change, no per-frame style write; it takes any {vx,vy} actor and wrapper, so the floor avatar (the report’s later sentences, a later chunk) reuses it. Low VFX / Reduce Motion / Battery saver / prefers-reduced-motion: fm-still, the sprite holds still. Probe tools/probe_flooranim.py 1/7 on the old tree, 7/7 after. At release: no player-page edit (the page already says the creatures roam the floor; this is how they look doing it).
