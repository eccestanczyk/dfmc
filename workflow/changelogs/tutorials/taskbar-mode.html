TASKBAR MODE — TEAM NOTES

The team record for the public page tutorials/taskbar-mode.html: its dated changelog (newest first) and the implementation notes that never go on the player page (D 2026-11-03: the codex is player facing). The shell's own rules and display conventions: workflow/system-client-shell.html.

Changelog

  • 2026-11-11 — Taskbar Mode: a BAG FULL / SOUL BAG FULL plate when a bag is full; a click goes back to the window with that bag open (bug#1255; dfmc-client bug run 2026-11-10, chunk fullbag). On dev. The page sends full:{kind,lbl,cnt,tip} in the strip snapshot (null while there is room); steam/strip.html #bf draws it at the band’s bottom-left, sized with the band and never faded; a click sends dfmc:strip:bagfull, main ends Taskbar Mode and relays cmd bagfull, and the page opens the full bag. The full record: workflow/changelogs/tutorials/inventory.html. Probe tools/probe_01255_fullbag.py. At release: on tutorials/taskbar-mode.html, after the sentence naming the strip’s buttons, add: “When your inventory or soul bag is full, the strip shows BAG FULL or SOUL BAG FULL; click it to go back to the game with that bag open.”
  • 2026-11-10 — Taskbar Mode: a battle starts with the team walking up to the enemies, who come in from the right one at a time (bug#1247; dfmc-client bug run 2026-11-10, chunk taskbarwalk). SHIPPED IN 0.98. D: “When starting battle in taskbar mode, currently monsters are teleporting in to their battle positions. The player party needs to walk up to the monsters until they are in position. That is, they appear one at a time from the right side of the screen as if they had always been there waiting.” The enemies used to fade in where they stand. Now, as a battle starts, the team walks up and the ground scrolls while the three enemies slide in from beyond the strip’s right edge, nearest first, as if they had been standing there; under a second in all, so the strip never runs behind the fight. With Low VFX, Battery Saver, Reduce Motion or the system’s reduced motion everyone stands in place at once. Mechanism in workflow/system-client-shell.html. Probe tools/probe_01247_walkin.py. At release: on tutorials/taskbar-mode.html, in the lead after “keeps autoplay running along your taskbar while the main window is hidden.”, add: “When a fight starts, the team walks up to the enemies as they come into view from the right.”
  • 2026-11-10 — Taskbar Mode: resizing shows an outline of the new size and the strip takes it on release; the floor reads F18 and fades with the buttons (bug#1247; dfmc-client bug run 2026-11-10, chunk taskbarsize). SHIPPED IN 0.98. D: “When resizing, instead of real time shuffling the contents of the taskbar mode window, show a sillouette of the new frame size you are resizing, and then when the player releases the drag the window actually resizes to the new size.” and “Instead of it saying FLOOR #, it should say F#. That text needs to resize together with the window, and it needs to also fade out.” Dragging an edge now leaves the strip as it is and draws an iron-framed outline of the size it will take (the same width-to-height shape as before, D’s 2026-11-09 order); letting go resizes the strip once. The floor label is short (F18; each language its own short form), grows and shrinks with the strip, and shows and fades with the AUTO / ON TOP / WINDOW buttons. Mechanism in workflow/system-client-shell.html. Probe tools/probe_01247_size.py. At release: in the page’s Taskbar Mode paragraph, after “The strip shows the floor, the streak and the autoplay state”, add: “The floor shows as a short F number in the strip’s corner, sized with the strip, and it fades away with the buttons when the mouse leaves. Drag an edge to resize the strip: an outline shows the new size, and the strip takes it when you let go.”
  • 2026-11-10 — Taskbar Mode: the strip’s buttons fade out every time the mouse leaves, after a resize or a click too (bug#1247; dfmc-client bug run 2026-11-10, chunk taskbarfade). SHIPPED IN 0.98. D: “on taskbar mode the buttons are not reliably fading out after unhovering the taskbar mode window. It happened after I resized it”. The #1233 fade relied on the strip window seeing the mouse leave, and it missed three ways: a resize or drag that ended with the cursor already outside (the strip never heard it leave, and the band stayed solid, so clicks under it did not pass through either); a fast exit; and a click on AUTO / ON TOP, which left that button focused and held the row up until something else took focus. Now the shell itself checks where the mouse is while the buttons are up and fades them 0.7 s after it is off the strip, and a click no longer pins them; a keyboard or controller focus still shows them. Mechanism in workflow/system-client-shell.html. Probe tools/probe_01247.py. At release: no page edit — the page’s sentence that the buttons fade away when the mouse leaves (from the 2026-11-09 entry) now holds every time.
  • 2026-11-09 — Taskbar Mode: the strip’s buttons show only while the mouse is over it; a resize keeps the strip’s shape (ui#1233 and D’s order with it; dfmc-client bug run 2026-11-09, chunk taskbar). On dev. (1) #1233: AUTO / ON TOP / WINDOW sat over the strip at all times. They now rest hidden and fade in (0.16 s) while the pointer is anywhere over the strip window; when the cursor leaves they stay 0.7 s, then fade out (0.28 s). A focused button (keyboard, controller) keeps the row up, and a tap on a touch screen shows it for 4 s. (2) D, same day: “resizing the taskbar-mode window must keep the same aspect ratio”. Any edge or corner now resizes the band at one width:height (the ratio both teams need at the smallest band, about 4.4:1; the strip therefore opens about 20 px wider than before); a side edge grows the band upward so the strip stays on the taskbar, and the minimum and maximum are those of #1203. Mechanism in workflow/system-client-shell.html. Probe tools/probe_01233.py. At release: on tutorials/taskbar-mode.html, where the page names the strip’s buttons, add “The buttons appear when you move the mouse over the strip and fade away when it leaves.”; where the edge-resize sentence sits (added from the 2026-11-06 entry), add “The strip keeps its shape as it grows or shrinks.”
  • 2026-11-06 — Taskbar Mode: the strip resizes by its edges; its fight animations are the game’s (bug#1203, bug#1207; dfmc-client lane shellstrip). On dev. (1) #1203: the strip was a fixed size. Its band’s four edges and corners now resize it, down to a band of 64 px (a 40 px sprite, the team still readable) and up to 360 px; it is never narrower than both teams need at its height, and it keeps its size until the client closes. (2) #1207: the strip drew one lunge of its own for every attacker and nothing on the one hit. It now plays the battle’s own animations - the attacker’s lunge or cast, the target’s hit jolt (once per hit on a multi-hit), shudder or buff bob, the hit-stop hold - with the game’s timing, and a fallen unit greys out as it does on the battle screen. Mechanism in workflow/system-client-shell.html. Probe tools/probe_shellstrip.py. At release: on tutorials/taskbar-mode.html, step 2, after the sentence about bringing the window back, add “Drag an edge of the strip to make it bigger or smaller; it stops before the team gets too small to see.”
  • 2026-11-06 — Taskbar Mode ends when the game window comes back (bug#1202, ui#1206; dfmc-client lane shellwin). On dev. The strip's WINDOW button restored the game and left the strip open beside it, and a click on the game in the Windows taskbar did the same; now either closes the strip, and the game returns windowed or fullscreen as it was left (steam/main.js stripEnd(); workflow/system-client-shell.html has the mechanism). Probe tools/probe_shellwin.py. At release: on tutorials/taskbar-mode.html, step 2, replace “click it to bring the window back.” with “its WINDOW button brings the game back and closes the strip — so does clicking the game in the Windows taskbar. The game comes back windowed or fullscreen, as you left it.”
