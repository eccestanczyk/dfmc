TASKBAR MODE — TEAM NOTES

The team record for the public page tutorials/taskbar-mode.html: its dated changelog (newest first) and the implementation notes that never go on the player page (D 2026-11-03: the codex is player facing). The shell's own rules and display conventions: workflow/system-client-shell.html.

Changelog

  • 2026-11-14 — Taskbar Mode: battle text, effects and spoil icons; the health bars stay inside the frame (bug#1277; dfmc-client bug run 2026-11-14, chunk tb-strip). On dev. The strip now floats the game’s WEAK / status / KO text with the damage numbers, over the iron frame; shows each move’s effects; sits the team higher so the health bars stay inside the frame at the smallest size; and drops show their item and currency icons. Implementation notes: workflow/system-client-shell.html, 2026-11-14 tb-strip entry. At release: on tutorials/taskbar-mode.html, replace “each item with its name over it.” with “each item with its icon and its name over it.” and add after that sentence: “In a fight the strip shows each move’s effects and the same floating text as the game window: the damage, WEAK, new statuses and KO.”
  • 2026-11-14 — Taskbar Mode: the fight plays on the game’s own clock, hits and numbers land together (bug#1277; dfmc-client bug run 2026-11-14, chunk tb-sync). On dev. D: “taskbar mode the animations are still out of sync with the attacks, needs to be the same as in regular window mode battles”. #1207 gave the strip the battle tile’s keyframes but kept the strip’s own clock: an act began when the page’s 100 ms snapshot pump delivered it (0–100 ms late), ran past the game’s act window, and its damage number was an hp difference drawn on arrival, ahead of the hit. Now runActs stamps act.at / act.win, stripSnap sends them with the fct hit record, and steam/strip.js starts the act when the game did, ends it where the game’s window ends, lands each number at the impact (a multi-hit’s per-hit numbers then the total “d ×N”, as the tile’s floating combat text) and steps a multi-hit’s hp bar hit by hit. The shell record: workflow/system-client-shell.html. Probe tools/probe_01277_tbsync.py 3 / 13 on the old tree → 16 / 0. At release: on tutorials/taskbar-mode.html, after the walk-up sentence (“When a fight starts, ... from the right.”), add: “In a fight the strip keeps time with the game window: each attack, hit and damage number lands on the same beat, and a multi-hit counts down the health bar hit by hit.”
  • 2026-11-11 — Taskbar Mode: the resize outline ships in the app, and the loot drops onto the ground from the defeated foes (bug#1271; dfmc-client bug run 2026-11-11, chunk taskbar). SHIPPED IN 0.100. Resize: #1247’s silhouette window loads steam/strip_ghost.html, but steam/build.ps1 copies a fixed file list into the package and that file was not on it, so the 0.99 app opened an empty transparent window while an edge was held; the file is on the list now, and tools/probe_01271.py checks that every file main.js and its pages load is on it. Drops: #1189’s loot list over the foes is gone; steam/strip.js drawPost pops each item (up to 5) out of the defeated foe nearest its landing spot, on a gravity arc with one damped bounce, a ground shadow and a short glint in the item’s colour, 90 ms apart; it rests on the ground line with its name over it, scrolls away with the band as the party walks on and fades with the victory display. Low VFX / reduced motion: the items lie on the ground at once. Manual and autoplay battles alike (one snapshot path). The web build has no strip (its MENU plate says desktop only). Probe tools/probe_01271.py 1 / 13 on the old tree → 14 / 0. At release: on tutorials/taskbar-mode.html, after the BAG FULL sentence, add: “When a battle is won, the loot drops out of the defeated monsters onto the ground, each item with its name over it.”
  • 2026-11-11 — Taskbar Mode: a BAG FULL / SOUL BAG FULL plate when a bag is full; a click goes back to the window with that bag open (bug#1255; dfmc-client bug run 2026-11-10, chunk fullbag). SHIPPED IN 0.99. The page sends full:{kind,lbl,cnt,tip} in the strip snapshot (null while there is room); steam/strip.html #bf draws it at the band’s bottom-left, sized with the band and never faded; a click sends dfmc:strip:bagfull, main ends Taskbar Mode and relays cmd bagfull, and the page opens the full bag. The full record: workflow/changelogs/tutorials/inventory.html. Probe tools/probe_01255_fullbag.py.   • 2026-11-10 — Taskbar Mode: a battle starts with the team walking up to the enemies, who come in from the right one at a time (bug#1247; dfmc-client bug run 2026-11-10, chunk taskbarwalk). SHIPPED IN 0.98. D: “When starting battle in taskbar mode, currently monsters are teleporting in to their battle positions. The player party needs to walk up to the monsters until they are in position. That is, they appear one at a time from the right side of the screen as if they had always been there waiting.” The enemies used to fade in where they stand. Now, as a battle starts, the team walks up and the ground scrolls while the three enemies slide in from beyond the strip’s right edge, nearest first, as if they had been standing there; under a second in all, so the strip never runs behind the fight. With Low VFX, Battery Saver, Reduce Motion or the system’s reduced motion everyone stands in place at once. Mechanism in workflow/system-client-shell.html. Probe tools/probe_01247_walkin.py. At release: on tutorials/taskbar-mode.html, in the lead after “keeps autoplay running along your taskbar while the main window is hidden.”, add: “When a fight starts, the team walks up to the enemies as they come into view from the right.”
  • 2026-11-10 — Taskbar Mode: resizing shows an outline of the new size and the strip takes it on release; the floor reads F18 and fades with the buttons (bug#1247; dfmc-client bug run 2026-11-10, chunk taskbarsize). SHIPPED IN 0.98. D: “When resizing, instead of real time shuffling the contents of the taskbar mode window, show a sillouette of the new frame size you are resizing, and then when the player releases the drag the window actually resizes to the new size.” and “Instead of it saying FLOOR #, it should say F#. That text needs to resize together with the window, and it needs to also fade out.” Dragging an edge now leaves the strip as it is and draws an iron-framed outline of the size it will take (the same width-to-height shape as before, D’s 2026-11-09 order); letting go resizes the strip once. The floor label is short (F18; each language its own short form), grows and shrinks with the strip, and shows and fades with the AUTO / ON TOP / WINDOW buttons. Mechanism in workflow/system-client-shell.html. Probe tools/probe_01247_size.py. At release: in the page’s Taskbar Mode paragraph, after “The strip shows the floor, the streak and the autoplay state”, add: “The floor shows as a short F number in the strip’s corner, sized with the strip, and it fades away with the buttons when the mouse leaves. Drag an edge to resize the strip: an outline shows the new size, and the strip takes it when you let go.”
  • 2026-11-10 — Taskbar Mode: the strip’s buttons fade out every time the mouse leaves, after a resize or a click too (bug#1247; dfmc-client bug run 2026-11-10, chunk taskbarfade). SHIPPED IN 0.98. D: “on taskbar mode the buttons are not reliably fading out after unhovering the taskbar mode window. It happened after I resized it”. The #1233 fade relied on the strip window seeing the mouse leave, and it missed three ways: a resize or drag that ended with the cursor already outside (the strip never heard it leave, and the band stayed solid, so clicks under it did not pass through either); a fast exit; and a click on AUTO / ON TOP, which left that button focused and held the row up until something else took focus. Now the shell itself checks where the mouse is while the buttons are up and fades them 0.7 s after it is off the strip, and a click no longer pins them; a keyboard or controller focus still shows them. Mechanism in workflow/system-client-shell.html. Probe tools/probe_01247.py. At release: no page edit — the page’s sentence that the buttons fade away when the mouse leaves (from the 2026-11-09 entry) now holds every time.
  • 2026-11-09 — Taskbar Mode: the strip’s buttons show only while the mouse is over it; a resize keeps the strip’s shape (ui#1233 and D’s order with it; dfmc-client bug run 2026-11-09, chunk taskbar). SHIPPED IN 0.97. (1) #1233: AUTO / ON TOP / WINDOW sat over the strip at all times. They now rest hidden and fade in (0.16 s) while the pointer is anywhere over the strip window; when the cursor leaves they stay 0.7 s, then fade out (0.28 s). A focused button (keyboard, controller) keeps the row up, and a tap on a touch screen shows it for 4 s. (2) D, same day: “resizing the taskbar-mode window must keep the same aspect ratio”. Any edge or corner now resizes the band at one width:height (the ratio both teams need at the smallest band, about 4.4:1; the strip therefore opens about 20 px wider than before); a side edge grows the band upward so the strip stays on the taskbar, and the minimum and maximum are those of #1203. Mechanism in workflow/system-client-shell.html. Probe tools/probe_01233.py. At release: on tutorials/taskbar-mode.html, where the page names the strip’s buttons, add “The buttons appear when you move the mouse over the strip and fade away when it leaves.”; where the edge-resize sentence sits (added from the 2026-11-06 entry), add “The strip keeps its shape as it grows or shrinks.”
  • 2026-11-06 — Taskbar Mode: the strip resizes by its edges; its fight animations are the game’s (bug#1203, bug#1207; dfmc-client lane shellstrip). SHIPPED IN 0.96. (1) #1203: the strip was a fixed size. Its band’s four edges and corners now resize it, down to a band of 64 px (a 40 px sprite, the team still readable) and up to 360 px; it is never narrower than both teams need at its height, and it keeps its size until the client closes. (2) #1207: the strip drew one lunge of its own for every attacker and nothing on the one hit. It now plays the battle’s own animations - the attacker’s lunge or cast, the target’s hit jolt (once per hit on a multi-hit), shudder or buff bob, the hit-stop hold - with the game’s timing, and a fallen unit greys out as it does on the battle screen. Mechanism in workflow/system-client-shell.html. Probe tools/probe_shellstrip.py. At release: on tutorials/taskbar-mode.html, step 2, after the sentence about bringing the window back, add “Drag an edge of the strip to make it bigger or smaller; it stops before the team gets too small to see.”
