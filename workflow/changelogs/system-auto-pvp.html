  • 2026-10-22 — the queue is never empty: the defender pool, the power window and the TOWER ECHO (Tower rulings form card T6 part 5, D 2026-10-18; 0.89-4 board card Q15 "Queue never empty"). LIVE on release since 2026-10-23 (0.89). Every account whose best floor (the server's ratchet, now written on the daily defense snapshot as maxf) has reached the Auto PvP rung and whose fold built a defense team is in the defender pool - the server's own snapshot, never the attacker's client; a snapshot below the rung is never served. The mint is power-filtered: the Worker computes the attacker's power from his stored save (src/power.js computePower, the T8 rule - the same number the fold wrote on every defender's snapshot) and the pick stays inside a window of ×1.25 around it, widening to ×1.5 and ×2 when the pool is thin; the rating band then picks inside the window as before. When no human stands in the widest window the match is against a TOWER ECHO: a house team the Worker builds from the codex for the attacker's ratchet floor (a class line, a level around the floor, two companions native to the floor band from creatures.csv, built by the same src/team.js slice every human defense is built by, stood at the T4 wild-HP band for that floor), seeded by attacker + day + idem so the same attacker meets varied but reproducible echoes. An echo match costs the attack, runs and settles like any other (replay, forfeit, expiry) and carries bot:1 on the row and the response - but writes no ladder result for any human: no rating or W/L move for the attacker, no row for the house, no season credit for that week. The arena card names the defender TOWER ECHO [ECHO]. An unreadable attacker power, or a Worker that carried no echo (codex tables missing), keeps the old unfiltered pick, so a missing table never empties the queue.   • 2026-10-01 — the server builds both teams from the saved roster (D ruling, Rulings board: row teamstats, “SERVER AUTHORITATIVE, EVERYTHING”). LIVE on release since 2026-10-23 (0.89). The pinned teams used to be a team summary the game client wrote into the save (stats, gear package, moves and their effects); the attacker's at the mint, the defender's at the daily fold. One edit of that summary before a save was a forged team the server then verified a fight against. Honest teams are unchanged, number for number; a forged summary changes nothing. Nothing changes for players; an older client keeps working. Defense teams pinned before the deploy are replaced at the next daily fold.
  • 2026-10-01 — a disproved result is void, and a week’s reward needs a match that week (D rulings, Rulings board: card apvp, row season). LIVE on release since 2026-10-23 (0.89). Void: since the 08-16 burn-in, a result the server’s check disproved was flagged and still paid as claimed: the attacker took the rating and the honest defender lost it. Every benign cause of a flag has since been fixed, so a flag now means a cheat or a client bug. Such a match is now refused: the ticket stays spent, no rating or win/loss record moves on either side, and the automatic report is still filed (one per reason per day, with the running count and the last match). The match-end note reads VOIDED instead of a victory. Season attendance: the reward used to require one settled match ever, attack or defense, so a top-ten account kept collecting every week after a single fight. A week’s reward now requires an attack you started in that week that closed as a real result (a void does not count); a missed week, including one closed late by the catch-up settle, pays only those who attacked in it.

  • 2026-09-30 — the finish door owns the starting board and ultimates are once per battle (project review F187, F188). SHIPPED IN 0.92 or earlier; LIVE on release since 2026-10-01 (0.88).. The re-sim used to take the attacker’s submitted starting board as-is — hit points, knock-outs, statuses and the fight-frozen Attack/Defense anchors were neither checked nor reset — so a claim with the defense at 1 HP, already knocked out, or an attacker at a hundred times his Attack re-simulated as a win and settled clean. Now every fight replays from the server’s own round 0: every unit at full HP, alive, no statuses, no ultimate spent, and an empty slot stays empty. The Attack/Defense anchors are re-derived from the pinned team slice with the client’s own formula and pinned (an attacker creature’s Defense may sit anywhere its own conditional Defense souls allow), and the gear package, seat, side and hero flag must match the slice — any mismatch fails the units pin. Ultimates: the engine now refuses a queued ultimate once that unit has spent it, as the class codex has always said (once per battle); the finish door strips any ultimate row from a creature (only the two heroes carry one, from the class token), and a replay that queues an ultimate on a spent or row-less unit ends there. Both are flagged exactly like a units-pin failure (paid during burn-in, reported) — the burn-in policy is unchanged. Honest play is unchanged: the client already starts every fight this way and only offers an ultimate while it is unspent.   • 2026-09-29 — the finish door checks cooldowns (project review F056). SHIPPED IN 0.92 or earlier; LIVE on release since 2026-10-01 (0.88).. The shared battle engine never read a queued move’s cooldown — only the client’s buttons and autoplay brain honoured it — and the finish door replays the rounds the attacker submits, so a doctored claim that fired a cooldown-3 Nuke every round re-simulated as a win and settled. The loop: Queue / Fight / Finish rows

QueueSpends one attack and mints a match: the server picks the defender from every snapshotted account at the Auto PvP rung whose power is inside a window around yours (×1.25, widening to ×1.5 and ×2 when the pool is thin), or a TOWER ECHO house team built for your best floor when nobody is in it (no rating moves either way on an echo); pins the seed, pins both teams, and starts a 20-minute clock. FightManual or autoplay, exactly like any other battle. The defense is piloted by the defender's own autoplay settings, and Competitive's damage ramp applies. FinishThe server re-simulates the whole fight from the pinned seed and teams. Agreement settles the match; both ratings move. Cooldowns are the server’s: every move starts the fight ready, the re-sim tracks each one itself, and a submitted round that fires a move still cooling is flagged, never settled. A claim the server disproves is void: a roster that does not match the teams pinned at the queue, an illegal act in the replay, or a re-sim that reaches another winner — the ticket stays spent, neither rating moves, the match counts for nobody, and a report is filed for D.

The loop: Abandon row

AbandonA match left unfinished past 20 minutes settles as a defense win. The attack is not refunded — walking away from a bad matchup is not free.

The snapshot paragraph

Your defense is a copy of your team taken by the daily fold — the same team you last saved, with its gear, levels and moves frozen as they were. Your attack is the team you have equipped when you queue (D ruling 2026-08-25: “The team you take to async pvp needs to be the one you currently have equipped. The daily snapshot is for the DEFENDING team”). The client saves before it queues, and the server reads the attacking team from that save and pins it for the match — the client never names its own team — so both sides of every match are still a fixed, knowable thing.

Weeks paragraph

There are no seasons (D, Tower rulings T33, 2026-10-22). The ladder runs on weeks: the week closes on a fixed Sunday 00:00 UTC cutoff, the same clock as the daily reset, the standings are cut, the top ten are paid, and every rating carries on unchanged — nothing resets.
