Implementation notes

  • UNLOCK_SYSTEMS in play/app.js is the key list (class_soul, hatch_all, dismantle_all, souls, devour, auto_pvp, auto_optimise, breeding, scrolls, grading, upgrade_all, trader, classes, competitive_pvp, autoplay + transfer); the markup binds lk.<key> before the csv loads (T6 part 1).
  • Columns: Anchor is the control the arrow points at, Via the window path to it, Ready the condition besides the floor (an egg in the bag, a soul, a pair), Line the one-line tip.
  • The floor is judged on the server's save; the client only paints the lock. POST /api/unlock is the door for the paid class unlocks on Classes, not for this ladder.

The ladder (intro paragraph)

The table is codex/unlocks.csv, which the client reads at boot; the floor numbers here are the game's. Single hatch and single dismantle are open from floor 1 (ruling 2026-09-09), and the soul bag window is open from floor 1 so eggs can hatch (2026-10-23).

The opening and the arrows

  • Floors 1–5 are played by hand — the scripted opening (T6 part 4) teaches the fight, the portal and gear grading; Autoplay opens when the first battle ends, won or lost (#1143, D 2026-11-02) — the one rung that is not a floor.
  • One arrow per rung (T6 part 3) — when a system opens, an arrow with its label points at the control, follows it across windows, and goes away the first time you click that control. It never blocks play.
  • A locked control is shown locked, with the floor that opens it, so you know what is coming.
  • The class soul is yours from creation — its UPGRADE opens with the souls rung at floor 12 (D: “class soul earlier — at the beginning”).
  • Breeding pens open with Ichor, not floors, once breeding itself is open at 25 (codex/pen_unlocks.csv, Breeding).
