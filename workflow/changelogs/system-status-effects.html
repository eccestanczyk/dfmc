Team notes (moved off the public page 2026-11-04)

From the public section “Identity: a status is its ability”

A status is identified by the ability that applied it, not by the stat it moves. Every buff payload a move produces is stamped at build time with:

  • key = 'ab:' + Move_ID — e.g. ab:M-STORMFATHER-4. This is the identity the engine matches on.
  • name = the ability's own stage name (Stormcall / Call the Storm / Convocation of Storms). This is what the player sees on the status chip.

From the public section “The named statuses”

The family of each name is the STATUS_ITYPE map and the per-turn fractions the DOT map, both in src/battle.js, built into play/battle_module.js; they pick the chip icon and the tooltip family too.

Signature mechanics cannot be removed (reports #117 / #118)

Status: on release. D's ruling, 2026-08-12: “Boss buffs / unique mechanics cannot drop. They need to be permanent for the fight and impossible to remove.”

The engine already promised this and did not keep it. A boss's unique rule is carried as a status with the sig flag: it is painted with the skull icon, and its own tooltip already reads “This cannot be removed.” But the predicate that answers “is this a buff?” returns TRUE for a signature status — correctly, it is a buff — and four separate paths acted on that answer without ever asking whether the thing was allowed to be taken:

  • Remove buffs stripped it outright.
  • Steal buffs took it off the boss and put it on the player, so the boss lost its mechanic and the player gained one.
  • Buff shortening pulled its duration down — measured 99 turns to 94 in one hit. Shortening is removal with extra steps: a mechanic that expires early did not last “permanent for the fight”.
  • The Unfinished Work's own round rule, which clears the party's buffs, wiped a signature status off a party member too.

Why it was seen only a few times. Every one of those needs a player carrying a strip, steal or shorten effect. A bare account has none, which is why the report never reproduced and was twice recorded as “could not reproduce”.

The rule now. One predicate — is this buff strippable — is asked at every removal site. A signature status still READS as a buff everywhere else: it shows in the buff row, it satisfies “target has a buff” conditions, and it is described in tooltips exactly as before. It is simply never a legal target for removal, theft or shortening. Authored counterplay is untouched — Sethek's second rising is still cancelled by killing his escort first (#116), because that is the boss's own written rule rather than a status being taken off him.

Engine: isStrippableBuff() and applyFxTo() in src/battle_round.js, built into play/battle_module.js, whose resolveRound runs the fight; the SIGFX rules in play/app.js. The applyFxTo still defined in play/app.js has no caller since 3.7 and is not the live path. Gate: tools/probe_sigperm.py 15/15, and 8/15 against the build it replaces — the failing run reproduces all four losses on a real client before fixing any of them, and every check has a control proving ordinary buffs are still removable.

Thorns — what is reflected

Thorns comes from two places and both follow one rule: a status on the unit (a move rider such as Quillhide, carrying thornsPct) and the soul package (the character’s own thorns souls, plus team thorns on every ally). Each fires once per hit taken and deals its share straight to the attacker’s HP, on the damage line as “N reflected”. Retaliation ultimates are a different mechanic and are not thorns.

The share is of the damage the unit actually lost on the hit — after its own defence, and never more than it had left. (#771, D ruling A, 2026-09-07 — LIVE on release since 2026-09-08 (0.71).; this retracts the 2026-09-02 rule below.) The 09-02 rule read the hit as the attacker put it out, before the target’s defence, so that a tank reflected as much as a paper target; against an armoured target it did the opposite of its purpose: the floor-108 Talos (Divine, defence 9, its +50% Defense up) sent 15% of a 27k-attack creature’s raw output back — 28,790 — and killed a 3,862-HP attacker outright for landing a 1,722 hit. Under ruling A that same hit returns 258. Both sources, status and soul package; multi-hit summed; overkill counts only what the unit had. Gate tools/probe_thorns.py 5/5 through the real floor-108 boss party and the shared engine.

Retracted rule, kept for the record (LIVE on release 0.66 until 0.71): the share was of the hit as the attacker put it out — the engine computed the hit a second time against its reference defence (10 × STAT_SCALE) and thorns read that figure, so a tank reflected exactly what a paper target would off the same swing.

Before 2026-09-02 thorns read the damage actually taken — the reading ruling A returns to. A tank that mitigated a 2,000 hit to 100 reflected 4% of 100 — the souls were only worth carrying on a unit that took no damage, which is the one unit that never got to use them.
