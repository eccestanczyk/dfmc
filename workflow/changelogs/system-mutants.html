2026-09-16 — a mutant’s creature sheet wears its own skin, whatever the Codex says of its species (#922, D). ON DEV (0.81). The report, in Portuguese: mutants did not show their colour when equipped, from the items screen, nor in the creature information. The sheet was the one surface keyed on the species — it chose the mutant art from the Codex’s own mutant flag, which only a mutant hatch writes — while the inventory tiles, the equipment slot, the current-slot panel, the floor and the fight all ask the record itself. So a mutant made by devour or fusion, or one whose species the Codex never flagged, opened its sheet in ordinary colours from the bag tile and from the press-and-hold on its equipment card alike (they are one opener), and the inverse held too: a regular creature of a species you had hatched a mutant of wore the mutant palette on its sheet. When the sheet is opened on an individual, that individual now decides its art and its medal; a Codex-only sheet keeps the species reading, and a wild mutant’s sheet wears the palette it fights in. The equipped surfaces were already right, which is why the reporter saw the two disagree. Gate tools/probe_sheetmut.py 12/12, red 5/12.

2026-09-09 — a mutant in the soul picker finally wears its own skin (#793, D). LIVE on release since 2026-09-09 (0.73). The row in the soul picker has always been labelled “(Mutant)”, and the picture beside it was the ordinary art of that species — on every mutant, not just the one that was reported. The picker was the one surface that never got the mutant-aware art lookup the inventory tiles and the creature details window both use. Nothing about the art or the data was missing: all three of the reporter’s mutants had their recoloured images sitting in the codex the whole time. Gate tools/probe_mutsoul.py 5/5, red 4/5.

2026-08-29 — every mutant rebuilt from current art, and a line's stages now share one colour. Two faults, found together. Fifty-eight mutants predated the art they were made from — early creatures whose portraits were redrawn later, whose recolours were never redone — and they are all rebuilt. And the recolour itself is a deterministic hue rotation that was seeded from each creature's own identifier, so the three stages of a line were always going to land on three different colours: all 100 lines disagreed, 52 degrees apart on average and 96 at the worst. The rotation and the cluster split are now both taken from the line's first stage, so a colour is treated the same way all the way up the line. Across the roster the stages of a line now vary by 6 degrees where their own base art varies by 10. Charybdis has gone from 51° to 337°, against its stage one at 333°. Ten boss recolours were checked and were already correct.

Sixteen lines still vary more as mutants than their own art does, worst at 171°. That is the two-cluster split landing differently on stages whose palettes straddle its boundary, and closing it would be a change to the technique rather than a repair — left alone deliberately.

2026-08-29 — a mutant is drawn as one in the equipment window too. LIVE on release since 2026-09-01 (0.65). A player devoured a same-line mutant into his creature; it turned mutant in battle, but its icon in the equipment window kept the ordinary art. Devour had set the flag correctly and the battle was reading it — the equipment slot simply never asked. It looked its icon up in the item art table by name, which holds no creatures and knows nothing about mutants, while the resolver used everywhere else has always handled it. Both slot builders now go through that one resolver, so a creature in a slot is drawn the same way a creature is drawn anywhere.

  • 2026-08-21 — Mutants can no longer be dismantled, by any door (D order). LIVE on release since 2026-08-21 (0.58). The client’s shared yield gate refuses them everywhere — sheet, hammer, by-grade, all-filtered, Adam and Eve — and the ops bulk sweep carries its own refusal. Adam and Eve treats a Mutant like a levelled creature: never dismantled and never a bucket’s keeper, and its window names how many it is leaving. Gates: tools/probe_dismmutant.py 9/9, tools/probe_dismmutant_ops.py 4/4. Mechanics live on crafting.html.
  • 2026-08-04 — the wild-spawn roll now actually uses the table on this page (LIVE on release since 2026-08-04 (0.29)). It did not. deriveSlot was handed mutPctOf(), which returned the number of bosses the player had downed (0–10) and used it as a percentage. A count where a probability belonged. The spawn's own grade — computed on the line directly above — was ignored entirely. One units error produced both live symptoms at once: a new player with no bosses down rolled 0% and could never see a wild Mutant, while a player six bosses in rolled 6% of every spawn, about 0.6 per ten-slot floor. That is report #23, “3 shiny in under 5 min”, on floor 91. Wild spawns now roll MUTANT_CHANCE against their own grade exactly as documented above: on the 91–100 band (40% Epic / 60% Legendary) that is 0.056% per spawn, roughly 1 in 1,800, a 107× reduction from what was live.
  • 2026-08-04 — the Codex bonus is now actually wired (LIVE on release since 2026-08-08 (0.42)). It had been implemented on both roll paths and was never reachable: the zone→species map it depends on (zoneSpeciesMap) was defined and never called, so the value handed to the hatch roll and the spawn roll was always 0 and every “with Codex bonus” figure on this page was unattainable by any player. The Worker now attaches the map to both requests, the same way it attaches the spawn table, because the Durable Object must never fetch. A codex-data outage is not fatal on either path — the bonus simply falls back to 0. Scale note, measured against live saves: a zone tier counts as complete only when all 90 species appearing on that zone's floors are acquired, so the bonus is a genuine endgame reward — an account 109 species in completes zero tiers.

Team notes (moved off the public page 2026-11-04)

Original wording of passages rewritten for players (cut verbatim). Still on the public page and NOT editable there: the gen:mut-chance block's gen-note naming src/worker.js MUTANT_CHANCE / cxMutBonusOf / cxVoidBonusOf and tools/codex_tables.py — it is written by the generator, so the generator must drop it.

  • The Void Apex (floors 101–120) is an eleventh zone in floors.csv and counts as a tier of its own, but the zone half is capped at +0.10% on the server, so it is a spare: any ten of the eleven complete the half.

  • Creatures appear on floors across several zones (a creature's Floor_Found reads like 1;11;21;32), so each zone's set holds 69–87 creatures (the Creatures column below)

  • Acquired means hatched. Seeing a creature in the wild marks it Seen in the Codex but does not count toward a tier. The server stamps it: the stage 1 when it hatches, and each later form when a creature the server hatched evolves into it. A flag the client sets on its own counts for nothing.

  • Void stamps (#193, D ruling 2026-08-19): +0.001% per creature line stamped Void (the server stamps a line the first time a Void-grade creature of it sits in the bag, and the stamp stays after dismantling), capped at +0.10% for all 100 lines. Together +0.20%, which lifts the Void ceiling to 0.30%. Both halves apply to hatches and to wild spawns and are read from the stored save on the server.
  • Never dismantled — every dismantle door refuses a Mutant (D order 2026-08-21, LIVE on release since 2026-08-21 (0.58)): the creature sheet withholds its dismantle button, the hammer refuses the mark and says why, the by-grade and all-filtered sweeps skip them, Adam and Eve leaves them — neither dismantled nor a bucket’s keeper — and the server-side ops sweep spares them. crafting.html carries the full mechanics.
