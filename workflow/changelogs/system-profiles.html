Team notes (moved off the public page 2026-11-04)

The lines below stood on the public page until 2026-11-04 and are kept verbatim. The public page drops the CSV names, the achievements door and the ruling ids; both tables re-checked against badges.csv and achievements.csv at feeeee88.

Every player has a public profile, and every profile carries the badges the account has earned. Badges are the server's: it awards them when the fact becomes true in a save it accepted, and nothing on the device can mint one. On Steam the same facts also unlock Steam achievements.

  • Read-only and public — anyone on the board can see it; it writes nothing and mints nothing. There is no profile text to edit.

The badge list is codex/badges.csv. Each is awarded once, when the server sees the fact in your save; a badge the server has not awarded does not exist, whatever the device thinks. A Reset Progress wipes them with the rest of the climb (T61).

Dimensional Traveler is the one badge earned outside the Tower: a creature you sent through the Dimensional Rift reaching level 50 in Herumon Dungeon. The Dungeon reports it back; the Tower awards it (T48, 2026-11-03).

On the Steam build the same server-side facts unlock achievements (codex/achievements.csv, pushed through /api/steam/achievements). Most are badges under another name; three are not badges at all. Hidden ones do not show their description on Steam until earned.
