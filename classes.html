CLASSES

Six player classes, each a three-stage line that advances at class level 33 and 66. Aptitude sums S1 12 · S2 16 · S3 20 across Attack / Defense / Speed (max 10 per axis). Warrior, Mage and Archer are the starter lines; buying any class opens on floor 50 (Unlocks). Brute, Assassin and Professor are also sold in the premium shop’s CLASSES tab for 500 Wraithgold each. Every stage-3 class has a Mutant skin in the premium shop (400 Wraithgold, all six for 2040); it shows from stage 3, and you can buy it earlier. How to switch and advance: Classes tutorial.

Class ultimates

One per line, upgrading with stage. Learned at class level 10. Costs your character’s action, once per battle, and goes ahead of every normal action regardless of Speed (two ultimates in the same round go in random order). Autoplay fires it on the round your Ultimate setting names (Autoplay).

Class stages

A class line reaches stage 2 at class level 33 and stage 3 at class level 66. The stage-up is offered once the level is reached; until you accept it, the class keeps its previous stage’s stats, art and name — on your character and on the Character Power board alike. Every stage of every line has its own art.

Every class has its own level

Each class line levels on its own. Switching class keeps the level of the class you leave and loads the level of the class you take; a newly bought class starts at level 1. Stats, the ultimate, stage art, POWER and your PvP team all use the active class’s level. Gear level requirements use your account level, which every class shares. Each battle’s class XP goes to the class that fought it.

Switching class sends your eight character gear pieces to the bag, gems and runes loose beside them (Sockets). Each line keeps its own saved builds, which put its gear back in one click (Equipment).
