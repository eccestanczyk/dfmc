THE REPORT PANEL

Hub › Other Systems › The Report Panel

The report panel is how a player talks to the team from inside the game. It is the REPORT A BUG tile on the main menu and Contact Support under Settings › Legal & Support. A person reads every report.

What you fill in

  • Kind — Bug, Suggestion or Other. Pick Bug when the game did something it should not have; Suggestion when you want it to do something new.
  • Title and what happened — your own words. Where you were and what you pressed is the most useful thing you can write.

What rides along

AttachedWhy
The game's state when you opened the panelFloor, party, window, battle in progress — a snapshot taken at open time, so what you describe is what we see. Where the state has moved while you typed, the live value rides along too.
Your notices and any errorsThe notices list (the systems your climb has opened, the floor-101 letter), plus any warnings or script errors the game caught. There is no play log on your device, so nothing else from your play is attached. Long lists are trimmed from the oldest end.
The build idWhich version you were running.
No screenshotNothing is captured from your screen.
Your game is saved to the server before the report is sent, so the state the report describes is the state the server holds. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what a report contains and how long it is kept.

What happens to it

  • The report reaches the team, and a person reads it.
  • A bug is reproduced and then fixed. A suggestion that needs a design decision goes to the game's designer first.
  • You are not written back to from inside the game. Join the Discord (the invite is on the main menu) if you want to follow a report.
