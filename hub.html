The Tower Awaits

A dark-fantasy, creature-collecting, competitive turn-based RPG. Climb the 100 floors and the Void Apex above them; hatch, breed, and advance 312 monsters; upgrade equipment; and tune your builds to reach the top of the leaderboards. Auto-mode takes the grind out of leveling — but to compete, you sharpen your own strategies and outplay other players head-to-head.
