3. Storage on your device

The game stores data in your browser so it works; none of it is used for tracking, so no consent banner is shown (EU ePrivacy Directive Art. 5(3) exempts storage strictly necessary for a service you asked for).

  • ht_session cookie — your sign-in session, HttpOnly, sent only to the game server.
  • Browser local storage — a copy of your save for fast loading, your settings, the random device ID, and a short device play log used in bug reports.
  • This codex website keeps an optional admin flag and page caches in local storage, and registers a service worker so pages refresh quickly.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (EU/EEA, UK, Brazil, Japan, California and elsewhere) you can ask us to:

  • see the data we hold about you, and get a copy;
  • take it with you — Settings › Account & Data › Export My Data downloads your save as JSON straight away; ask us for the rest;
  • correct it (for example your character name);
  • delete it — Settings › Account & Data › Delete Account, or from a browser through delete-account.html (see Deleting your account below); you can also ask through Contact Support or [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL]. The in-game Reset Progress wipes progress only and is not a deletion;
  • object to, or restrict, processing based on legitimate interest;
  • withdraw any consent you gave, without affecting what came before.

Deleting your account

Confirming Delete Account (typing DELETE) signs you out on every device and schedules the deletion 7 days later. If you sign in during those 7 days the game shows the date and offers Cancel deletion, which restores everything as it was. After the 7 days the account is deleted for good, within a day:

  • deleted: the save and its history, every ledger and inventory record, Wraithgold and every other currency (bought or earned — it is not refunded), creatures, gear and pens, linked sign-ins, your name, your rows on every leaderboard and PvP ladder, your defence team, the five-day backup copies of your save, and your Adventure Log uploads;
  • kept without your account: the text of bug reports you sent (their play log, context and browser details are deleted), security flags and traffic-log rows (your Steam ID is removed from them), and a minimal receipt of each purchase (what, when, the amount and the store's reference, with no account attached), because tax and refund law requires it ([RECORD-KEEPING PERIOD, per tax law of GOVERNING LAW]).

Signing in with the same Steam account afterwards starts a brand-new account.

We answer within one month (GDPR) or the shorter period your law sets, and we will not treat you differently for using these rights. You can also complain to your data protection authority — for example your EU national authority, the UK ICO, Brazil's ANPD, or Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission. We do not sell personal information and do not respond to “Do Not Track” signals differently, because we do no tracking for them to switch off.

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. [AGE GATE — not built yet; see the backlog entry]. If you believe a child under 13 (or under the digital-consent age in your country) has an account, tell us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Sessions are random tokens checked on the server; saves live in a per-player database object that only the server can write. No system is perfect; if a breach puts your data at risk we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.
