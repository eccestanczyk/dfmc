EGGS AND HATCHING

Tutorials › Eggs and hatching

Eggs drop from fights and from the breeding pens. They wait in the soul bag until you hatch them into creatures.

1Eggs in the soul bag

MENU > SOUL BAG holds your creatures and your eggs (1). HATCH ALL (2) shows how many eggs are waiting.

2Hatch one egg

Click an egg and confirm (1): the creature inside is revealed and joins your soul bag.

3Hatch All

From floor 8 HATCH ALL (1) opens every egg in one go, egg after egg on the stage; before that, each egg is hatched by hand.
