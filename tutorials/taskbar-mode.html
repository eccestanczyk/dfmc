TASKBAR MODE AND THE STEAM CLIENT

Tutorials › Taskbar Mode and the Steam client

Herumon Tower runs in the browser at playdfmc.com and as a Windows client you download from playdfmc.com/download (the Steam build). The client adds Taskbar Mode: a thin strip that keeps autoplay running along your taskbar while the main window is hidden. When a fight starts, the team walks up to the enemies as they come into view from the right. These screens are from the client; the web build has no TASKBAR MODE plate.

1The TASKBAR MODE plate

Install the client from playdfmc.com/download and sign in - Steam signs you in by itself. In the client the MENU has one extra plate, TASKBAR MODE; click it to drop the game into the strip.

2The T key

T toggles Taskbar Mode from anywhere in the game, and the binding is listed under GAME SETTINGS > CONTROLS beside Toggle Autoplay. The strip shows the floor, the streak and the autoplay state; its WINDOW button brings the game back and closes the strip — so does clicking the game in the Windows taskbar. The game comes back windowed or fullscreen, as you left it. The buttons (AUTO, ON TOP, WINDOW) appear when you move the mouse over the strip and fade away when it leaves. When your inventory or soul bag is full, the strip shows BAG FULL or SOUL BAG FULL; click it to go back to the game with that bag open. When a battle is won, the loot drops out of the defeated monsters onto the ground, each item with its name over it. The floor shows as a short F number in the strip’s corner, sized with the strip, and it fades away with the buttons when the mouse leaves. Drag an edge to resize the strip: an outline shows the new size, and the strip takes it when you let go. The strip keeps its shape as it grows or shrinks, and it stops before the team gets too small to see.
