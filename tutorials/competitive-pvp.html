COMPETITIVE PVP AND RANKINGS

Tutorials › Competitive PvP and rankings

Competitive PvP is live and double-blind: both players present, 3v3 with your avatar and two creatures, best of three, moves chosen at the same time. Matchmaking opens at floor 60 and pairs by rating; a code lets you fight one chosen player instead.

1Queue from the right card

In the PVP ARENA the Competitive card is on the right: your rank and rating above one large MATCHMAKING plate. Click it to queue; while you wait it shows the elapsed time and a cancel, and the band widens the longer you wait.

2Challenge by code

CHALLENGE opens the code panel. MAKE A CODE gives you a code to hand to one player: if you wait for them you fight live, otherwise they get one Auto PvP attack on your defense. Got a code? Type it in and FIGHT.

3The rankings window

RANKINGS (from the menu or the arena) shows the PvP boards side by side - Auto PvP on the left, Competitive on the right - and a PVE tab for the climb. The boards are cut daily; click any name to open that player's profile.

4Weekly pay

Both ladders pay once a week to players who fought that week: rank 1 takes incense and bait for 30 days plus the medal, ranks 2-3 incense and bait, ranks 4-10 incense. A thin week (under ten players) pays half the days and no medal.
