Stat & Equipment Tables

Every equipment, modifier and progression number in one place: the stat and damage formulas, gear and enhancement grids, upgrade odds and costs, drops and income.

0 — Core formulas

Creature stat (Atk/Def):  Stat = (Core + Σ TagStats) × AxisMult × QM
                              Core = 0.6 × 7.5 × (level + 25)     AxisMult = (3 × axisGrade + 5) / 8
                              (a grade-5 creature at level 100: Core 562.5 × AxisMult 2.50)
Creature Speed (flat):             Speed    = axisGrade          (1-10, equals axis grade)
Creature HP:                       HP       = ((4 × level + 35) × (5 + DefenseGrade) / 11 × 7.5 + Σ Heart tags) × QM
Creature quality multiplier:       QM       = 0.80 + 0.04 × qualityGrade      (applies to Atk/Def/HP — NEVER Speed)

Character Attack:   ((Σ ItemStats(Attack)  + 0.25 × Core) × ClassRating × (1 + Σ increased%) + 188) × ClassSoulMult   ClassRating = (3 × stageStat + 5) / 8
Character Defense:  ((Σ ItemStats(Defense) + 0.25 × Core) × ClassRating × (1 + Σ increased%) + 188) × ClassSoulMult   (+188 = innate floor, post-multiplier)
                    ClassRating never falls below 1.00 (grade 1 ×1.00, grade 5 ×2.50, grade 10 ×4.375), so gear always delivers at least its printed value.
                    ClassSoulMult = the class soul's grade multiplier (Worn ×1.00 → Void ×1.20); it multiplies the finished number, floor included.
                    Characters carry 25% of Core as a level term.
Character HP:       ((4 × level + 35) × (5 + stageDefStat) / 11 × 7.5 × 1.5 + Σ flat HP items) × (1 + soul HP%) × ClassSoulMult
Character Speed:    the class stage's Speed grade — a per-class, per-stage value like Attack and Defense, never a constant. No gear sources; only in-battle stat stages move it.
Boss HP:            (5 × Floor + 150) × 7.5 for the floor 10–100 bosses (not the creature HP formula); the Void Apex bosses (floors 102–120) carry their own, larger HP.

Damage:  RawDamage   = ((2 × Level / 5 + 2) × MovePower × √(Attack / Defense)) / (130 / 7.5) + (15 + Attack / 50) / hits
         FinalDamage = RawDamage × Synergy/Anti × Random(0.9–1.1) × (1 + Σ increased%) × Π(more)
         No same-type damage bonus and no type-effectiveness multiplier — there is no type chart.
         Type interaction lives only in each move's Synergy and Anti clauses.

Stat aggregation (multiplier model). ItemStat = (implicit + Σ gems + Σ Flat enchants) × (1 + Σ rune % + Σ Increased% enchants) × (1 + (LOCAL soul − 1) + Σ More% enchants). Scrolls roll ONE enchantment on apply (class weights and value bands in section 5), and the roll can only take the stat the host item itself carries — a Defense item never rolls Attack. More is additive with the item's LOCAL soul multiplier rather than a separate factor. Duplicates are allowed and stack inside their bucket. There is no separate reroll action: applying another scroll over a socketed one rerolls it, drawing a fresh class AND value, and the enchantment it replaces is destroyed. Scrolls can never be unsocketed; gems and runes always return to the bag intact. Three maximum More rolls from T10 scrolls reach +87%; flat enchants add into the base bracket.

Heart — flat HP

110 × tier × GF, about 2× the Fang / Carapace grid — multiply the table above by 2.04. A point of Creature Defense is simply worth more than a point of Creature HP: the Defense tag is added to Core before the axis multiplier (1.0–4.375) and quality, while the HP tag is flat. The doubled coefficient buys parity in effective durability, not double the wall: low-Defense creatures and early floors favour Heart, high-Defense creatures and deep floors favour Carapace.

5 — Enchantment pool

One enchantment rolls when a scroll is applied. No Ink reroll and no reroll action: applying another scroll over a socketed one is the reroll, and the old enchantment is destroyed. The class is drawn Flat 45 / Increased 40 / More 15 on the stat the host item carries (Attack, Defense or HP; Creature Attack / Defense / HP on Tags). The value: the scroll's tier picks the band, and the scroll's grade bends the draw toward the top of it (position = u^(1/K) for a uniform roll u, K from 1.0 at Worn to 5.0 at Void). The host item's tier and grade do not enter, and there is no minimum grade. Example columns show the median roll.

7 — Creature quality multiplier

QM = 0.80 + 0.04 × qualityGrade — multiplies Attack, Defense, and HP. Never Speed. Wild and boss eggs cap at Legendary; Abyssal to Void eggs come from breeding only (an egg can still hatch a creature above its own grade). Wild creatures roll their own quality once at spawn, banded by zone: Legendary at most through floor 100, rising toward Void in the Void Apex above it.

QualityWornCommonUncommonRareEpicLegendaryAbyssalDivinePrimordialVoid
Multiplier ×0.84×0.88×0.92×0.96×1.00×1.04×1.08×1.12×1.16×1.20

Void vs Worn = ×1.20 / ×0.84 = 43% more stats at identical species, level, and gear.
