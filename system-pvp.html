PVP, TIMERS & PERSISTENCE

Other Systems › PvP, Timers & Persistence

Ranked 3v3 (your avatar + 2 creatures per side), best of 3, team locked at match start, and both players choose their moves at the same time without seeing the other’s. This page covers the PvP Arena screen, the move timers, crashes and disconnects, matchmaking, challenge codes and the leaderboards. Asynchronous Auto PvP has its own page.

The PvP Arena screen

Open it from the main menu (PVP ARENA). The arena opens at floor 15 and Competitive matchmaking at floor 60. Two queue cards sit side by side and fill the screen:

Move timers

Both sides pick all their moves for the round at the same time. The timers bound the round, chess-clock style, not each unit.

TimerValueRule
Round timer (PvP)30 sBoth players pick within it. Resolution/animation time does not count.
Personal bank (PvP)90 sDrawn from only after the 30 s round timer is spent, chess-clock style. Never refills during a game; resets between games of the Bo3.
On expiry—The autoplay AI picks for you — the same AI that runs PvE grinding, not “first move in the list.” Losing time costs quality, not the game outright.
Damage ramp (PvP)×1.5 @ 20 → ×3 @ 40+Replaces the 30-round hard cap and its tiebreak (2026-08-16). From round 20 every hit and every lingering damage tick, both sides, is multiplied: ×1.5 at 20, ×2 at 30, ×2.5 at 35, ×3 from 40 on and holding. A game ends only on a wipe — the ramp guarantees one, so no tiebreak is needed or exists. A hot ×-chip beside the round counter shows the live multiplier from round 20. PvE never ramps. Replays of Competitive games ramp identically — playback must re-produce the recorded fight.
PvENo timerMove timers are a PvP-integrity tool; against the AI there is nothing to protect and autoplay already exists for players who want speed. PvE battles instead get full crash-safe resume (below).

The timers and the damage ramp are the server’s; every match uses the same ones.

Crashes, reloads and Alt+F4

Closing the game never gets you out of a battle. Alt+F4, a crash and a power cut all lead to the same place.

RuleWhat it means for you
Resume, never exitOn next launch a PvE battle resumes at the top of the current round — same units, same state, same floor. Nothing is lost. In PvE the only ways out of a fight are to finish it or FORFEIT (a loss).
Same rollA round’s roll is fixed before its result is shown, so closing the game on a bad roll and reloading replays the identical roll. There is nothing to re-roll.
Locked-in rounds standOnce both sides have locked in, the round’s result is fixed. If that round kills your character, the death happens on resume — watching it or not changes nothing.
No escape before locking inClosing the game before you lock in just returns you to the same choice with the same information. Nothing advances without a locked-in round.
Auto PvPAn Auto PvP attack does not resume after a reload; see Auto PvP for what happens to an unfinished attack.

Disconnects

CaseRule
You drop out of a matchThe match does not pause and your clock keeps running. When your round timer and bank run out, the autoplay AI plays your side, exactly as on a timeout.
You come backReconnect at any time and you take control from the next round; the round the AI already played for you stands. There is no forfeit for being away: the AI plays your side to the end of the match unless you return, and the result counts for rating like any other.
Your opponent never returnsThe server finishes the match itself from the last round it resolved, both sides on autoplay, and pays the winner.

Matchmaking

  • MATCHMAKING puts you in the server’s queue, one entry per account. You stay queued until a match or CANCEL, even with the arena closed.
  • Pairing is by rating. The server looks for the nearest rating first and widens the search the longer you wait.
  • Match found. Both players press JOIN MATCH within 20 seconds. If one does not, the match is cancelled: whoever joined goes straight back into the queue, the other is dropped from it. The server then builds both teams (up to 30 seconds; the same rule applies if a team is not ready) and the battle starts.
  • Every opponent is a real player. Competitive has no bots and no stand-in opponents.
  • An Infinite Abyss fight comes first. While you are in one you stay queued but are not paired, and a match already found cannot be joined (“FINISH YOUR ABYSS FIGHT TO JOIN”) until that fight ends.
  • Errors show. A failed matchmaking call says so on screen and retries; three failures in a row stop the search (“MATCHMAKING UNAVAILABLE”).

Taking a match during a PvE fight

You can join a match in the middle of a PvE fight. That fight is paused, not forfeited: when you close the PvP result it comes back at the same round, with the same units, floor and roll, and the enemy is still standing. Nothing is lost. The one exception is an Infinite Abyss fight, which must end first (above).

Locking in

  • Neither player sees the other’s moves before choosing. Each side’s moves are sealed first and opened only once both sides have locked in.
  • You are shown as locked in only once the server has your moves. The lock-in button then reads MOVES LOCKED and the wax stamp appears on the decree — never before.
  • The round, bank and game readout sits directly under the turn-order button.

Fair play

  • The server builds both teams from each player’s saved roster — every stat, move and gear bonus. Nothing a client sends about its own team is used, and a creature the account does not own is not fielded: its seat stays empty.
  • The server runs the round. It keeps the round number, its deadline and both time banks, resolves each round itself, and plays an absent side’s round with the autoplay AI.
  • The result is the server’s. A claimed result that differs from the server’s is overruled, never paid.
  • Every move is checked: one of the unit’s four, off cooldown, a legal target. A revealed move that does not match its seal is refused.

Leaderboards

Four all-time boards in the RANKINGS window, updated once a day. Two tabs: PVP (Auto PvP | Competitive PvP) and PVE (Character Power | Highest Climb), each tab’s two boards side by side. Beside each name the team shows as thumbnails — the last team the player won with on the PvP boards, the team currently fielded on the PvE boards. Click a name to open that hero’s profile. There are no seasons and no realms, and the boards never reset; a player with no played match for 7 days drops off the two PvP boards (Inactivity, below).

Challenge by code

Once Competitive PvP is open you can ask the server for a code (6 characters, valid 60 minutes, one live code per player) and hand it to one player. If you are waiting in the queue for that code when they enter it, the two of you get an ordinary live Competitive match and the code is used up; while you wait with a code you are never matched with anyone else. If you are not waiting, their team fights your saved defense team as an Auto PvP attack: it costs them one attack, counts toward the limit of two attacks per defender per day, and the server replays and settles it. You cannot redeem your own code; an expired or unknown code is refused.

ButtonWhat it does
CHALLENGEIn the PvP Arena header; opens the challenge panel.
MAKE A CODEShows your code, the time it is valid until and a COPY button. NEW CODE replaces it.
WAIT FOR THEM LIVEPuts you in the Competitive queue for that code only (the Competitive card says so). Cancelling, making a new code or the code expiring ends the wait.
ENTER A CODE › FIGHTTakes a code (letters and digits; dashes and spaces are dropped, lower case is raised) and sends it. You get a live match, which opens like any matchmaking pop, or an Auto PvP fight against the owner’s defense, settled by the server.

A refusal is one plain line: your own code, no such code or expired, two attacks on this player today, no defense saved, no attacks left.

Inactivity

After 7 days without a played match you no longer show on the Competitive and Auto PvP boards. A Competitive match counts once it finishes; an Auto PvP attack counts once it settles and is not void. An account that only defends has played nothing. From idle week 2, each full idle week takes 5% off the rating, down to the starting rating (1000 Competitive, 1500 Auto) and never below it; a rating already at or under the start does not change. The decay is applied the next time your rating is read (a fight, the queue or the daily board update). Your next match starts from the decayed rating and resets the idle count.

Weekly pay

Only the Auto PvP board pays weekly; the Competitive board does not. You are paid only if you made at least one Auto PvP attack that week that settled and was not voided. Rank 1 gets incense and bait for 30 / 30 days plus the medal, ranks 2–3 incense and bait for 30 / 30 days, ranks 4–10 incense for 30 days. A thin week — fewer than 10 players with a settled attack that week — pays 50% of the days and no medal. Full rules on Auto PvP.
