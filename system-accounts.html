ACCOUNTS & SIGN-IN

Hub › Other Systems › Accounts & Sign-in

Your account is the login you sign in with. It is created the first time you sign in, and it lives on our server: your save and everything the account owns are kept there, not on your device. The game never stores a password of yours and never sees one: every sign-in goes through the provider's own page.

Signing in

  • One button per provider. The sign-in gate offers Steam, Google and Discord.
  • The Steam client signs you in by itself. Inside the Steam download you are signed in with your Steam session; there is no page to visit. The web build at playdfmc.com uses Steam's own sign-in page.
  • Signing in from an app. When the game runs from its own copy rather than from playdfmc.com (the Steam download), a Google or Discord sign-in opens in your system browser and comes back to the game with a one-time code, good for 60 seconds and once only. Only the game's own addresses can receive that code. Nothing changes on playdfmc.com.
  • Your identity is the provider's id (your Steam ID, Google account id or Discord id). That id is the account; the name you pick for your character is a separate thing (below).
  • Sign Out (Settings › Account & Data) saves the climb, ends the session and returns to the gate.

One climb, several logins

Link Account (Settings › Account & Data) shows every configured provider; a gold one is linked. Linking lets a second login open this same climb: sign in later with Google or Discord and you land on the same save, the same creatures, the same Wraithgold. A link is made from inside a signed-in session, so nobody can attach a login to your account without being in it. When you link from the Steam download or the app, the provider sign-in happens in your browser and nothing is linked yet: back in the game a Confirm the link window names the login and your hero, and only Link there attaches it — so a link someone else sends you can never tie your Google or Discord to their climb.

Linked Sign-ins, right under it, lists every login that opens this climb (provider and the end of its id) with an Unlink next to each one while you keep at least one other way in. Unlinking a login does not touch your climb; that login simply no longer opens it, and it can be linked again later, here or on another account.

If the login you link already has a climb of its own, the two accounts are merged, and a merge is not a reset: the server keeps one of the two climbs and records what the other one held. Decide which login is the main one before you link.

One window owns the save

Only one window may write your save at a time. Opening the game in a second tab, window or device hands ownership to the newer one and the older one stops with “This window no longer owns your save”. Nothing is lost: the save the server holds is the last one it accepted. Close the extra window and reload the one you want to keep playing in.

Your character's name

  • Chosen at character creation and claimed on the server: the name is trimmed, runs of spaces collapse to one, it is checked against the name filter, and a name another player holds is refused before the climb starts.
  • Inside the Steam client the field is pre-filled from your Steam persona, folded to the allowed character set; you can change it.
  • There is no rename in game. A name that needs changing is a report; it is done by hand.

Reset, export, delete

ControlWhat it does
Export My DataDownloads your save as a JSON file. Everything else the server holds about you is listed in the Privacy Policy.
Reset Progress (New Game)Resets everything: creatures, gear, floor, badges, boards, ratings, bought bag slots, boons, pending trades — the climb starts again at Floor 1, Lv 1 with a new character. Two things stay: the login itself, and any Wraithgold you paid real money for (paid money is never deleted by a reset). Free or earned Wraithgold goes with the rest.
Delete AccountTwo steps — what goes, then the typed word DELETE. The account is marked for deletion 7 days out and every device is signed out; signing in before the day offers to cancel. After it, the account and every linked login are gone. The delete-account page is the same flow from a browser.
