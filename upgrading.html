UPGRADING

No pity. No failstacks. No protection. Every attempt is a pure roll. Failure costs currency only — items are never destroyed or downgraded.

Grading up (Worn → Void)

One shared ladder for gear (paid in Scrap) and enhancements (paid in Dust / Essence / Ink). Same success rates, different currencies. Attempt cost scales with the item's tier: the T10 column is the full price, and lower tiers are discounted along the income curve — Cost(rung, T) = max(1, round(base × income(T) / income(10))) — so a rung costs about the same play time at every tier.

Soul grading (Worn → Void)

Class souls only — grading applies to souls slotted into the character's eight gear slots; creature-side souls do not grade. A class soul's grade is its own, independent of the source creature's quality grade, and is kept through the source line's advancement. All souls start Worn. SoulMult = 1 + (1/9) × (grade# − 1) multiplies the soul’s output (passive magnitude / granted move power) — Worn ×1.00 → Void ×2.00, nine equal steps. A creature soul has no grade of its own: it reads the source creature’s quality grade, inherited from the egg. Creatures are not upgraded with resources; they are bred and hatched, so that lever is raised by breeding for a better egg, never bought. See Equipment for the combined stage×grade table. Each attempt costs a fixed four-currency bundle per step (Worn → Common: 30 Scrap + 2 Dust + 2 Essence + 2 Ink), and every currency in the bundle is required — there is no substituting one for another, so 5,000 Scrap and no Dust is refused “NOT ENOUGH DUST”. Same success ladder as gear. Ichor is not part of the bundle.

Using the upgrade bench

  • ATTEMPT · cost is one attempt, charged the price the button shows. A failed attempt spends the cost and changes nothing else; when you are short, the button reads NEED N MORE <currency>.
  • While the forge plays an attempt, a click skips the animation; the next attempt needs a click on ATTEMPT once it has landed.
  • ALL-IN! opens a confirmation, then rolls the same step over and over, spending the cost each time, until it succeeds or you run out. It is decided in one go, then every attempt plays out on the bench: click to skip one, hold to run them fast. It reads ALL-IN · NOT ENOUGH and stays greyed when you cannot afford even one attempt.
  • The game server decides every attempt, never your device. Reloading or closing the game cannot re-roll a failed attempt or bring the materials back, and a dropped connection never charges an attempt twice. Applying a scroll works the same way: a reload cannot re-roll its enchantment.
  • Nothing is spent until the result arrives. If the server has not answered within about 12 seconds, the bench says so and shows the result when it arrives; if the connection never reached the server, nothing was spent. SETTLING THE LAST ATTEMPT — NOTHING WAS SPENT means the bench is saving the previous result first; that click costs nothing.
  • No pity. The grey “N failed in a row” line is a count only; every attempt rolls the same odds.
  • What the panel shows. The item is drawn in the grade it is now, its border and the alchemical circle behind it tinted that grade; the from → to row describes the next attempt. A success adds a short aura ring in the grade just reached.

Upgrading equipped gear in place

Clicking an equipment slot offers ▲ UPGRADE on the equipped piece: the same grade ladder, costs and success rolls as an attempt from the bag, resolved in place. Sockets and the scroll follow the item through the promotion.

Auto-optimize

The equipment header carries ⚙ AUTO-OPTIMIZE: it proposes the strongest legal loadout from what the player owns — gear slots and tags, plus a global re-assignment of gems and runes drawn from the bag and from everything currently socketed on equipped pieces. The displayed POWER number is the single metric; slots that could host several stats resolve to whichever combination of piece and stones scores highest. Hard rules: stat-match (a stone only enters a host of its own base stat), class-locked weapons are never proposed, creatures, souls and scrolls are never touched, stones on unequipped inventory gear are not cannibalized, and ties keep the current piece so a second run proposes nothing. Nothing changes without the preview's confirmation; the preview states the exact inventory delta and is blocked by the inventory guard if the bag cannot absorb it.

The target decides, and Overall Power breaks the tie. Optimize For (in Settings) maximises the chosen metric, but on a slot where that metric cannot tell the candidates apart — Attack on a Head or a Chest, say — it falls back to overall power instead of leaving the slot alone. Both must be beaten strictly, so a loadout that is already best changes nothing.
