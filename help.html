Account & saves

How do I sign in?

With your Steam account. The game sends you to Steam's own sign-in page; we never see your Steam password. Steam tells us your Steam ID, and that ID is your account. Google, Apple and Discord sign-in are planned and are not available yet.

Where is my progress saved?

On our server. The game keeps a copy in your browser so it can load fast, but the server's copy is the real one, and every save is checked by the server (Integrity). Cloud save cannot be switched off. You can play on any device by signing in with the same Steam account.

"This window no longer owns your save" — what happened?

Your account was opened in a second tab, window or device. Only one window may write your save at a time, so the older one stops. Close the extra window and reload the one you want to keep playing in. Nothing is lost: the save the server holds is the last one it accepted.

Can I rename my character?

Not in game — the name is set when you create the character. If your name needs changing, ask through Contact Support.

How do I get a copy of my data?

Settings › Account & Data › Export My Data downloads your save as a JSON file. For everything else we hold about you (see the Privacy Policy), ask through Contact Support.

How do I start over, or delete my account?

Reset Progress (New Game) wipes the save and puts you back on Floor 1. It does not delete the account, and Wraithgold you paid for survives a reset (Premium Shop). Deleting the account has no button yet; until it does, ask through Contact Support and it is done by hand. See the Privacy Policy for what deletion removes.

Playing

Does death cost me my creatures or items?

No. Death costs your win streak and nothing else; characters, creatures and items persist (Realms, Streak).

How do I set my team?

Your battle team is your character plus two creatures, and it is set in the equipment window: the two creature slots are equipment slots (Party).

What does Autoplay do, and can it get me banned?

Autoplay fights creatures on the current floor and climbs when the portal opens. It never does anything you could not do by hand and never enters a boss den. It is part of the game; using it is fine (Autoplay).

How does Auto PVP work?

You attack a snapshot of another player's team; they do not need to be online, and their side plays by their own autoplay settings. Ten attacks a day, the server decides the result, seasons close weekly (Auto PVP, PvP & leaderboards).

How does breeding work?

Put a pair in a pen and it runs on its own, eating Ichor and dropping an egg each time its bar fills (Breeding).

Where can I see what happened while I was away?

The Adventure Log keeps every hatch, floor clear, boss kill, death and battle on the account (Adventure Log).

Wraithgold & the shop

What is Wraithgold?

The premium currency. It buys timed boons, extra Auto PVP attacks and the premium breeding pens (Premium Shop). It is a licence to use in-game content, has no cash value, and cannot be traded or withdrawn — see the Terms of Service.

Can I buy Wraithgold with real money?

Not yet. When real-money purchases open, the price, what you get, the refund rules and — for anything with a random result — the odds will be shown before you pay.

Problems & settings

How do I report a bug?

Settings › Legal & Support › Contact Support, or the report button in the game. With your words the report carries a recent play log and the state of the game when you filed it, so we can see what you saw. No screenshot is taken. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what is sent.

The game is too loud, too bright, or flashes too much.

Settings has volume sliders and a mute, plus Reduce Motion and Reduce Flashing under Language & Accessibility (Settings).

The game says a new version is out.

Reload. Every open window is moved to the new build so all players run the same rules (Client Shell).
