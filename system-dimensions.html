DIMENSIONS

Other Systems › Dimensions

Five side worlds, each opened by its own key from the Premium Shop. A dimension is five floors of five battles, and every battle pays one resource — ten times what a battle on a tower floor of the same level is worth. Open now — buy a key in the Premium Shop, press USE on its row and confirm. Five floors of five battles pay the key’s resource; leaving or dying returns you to the floor you came from.

How it works

  • A key is the only way in. Keys cost Wraithgold and are bought in the Premium Shop. Using one opens a confirmation window; confirming takes you to the dimension and consumes the key.
  • Five floors, five battles each, always upward. Win five battles and you climb to the next floor. There is no going down, no kill limit, and nothing respawns.
  • One resource per dimension. Every win pays that dimension’s resource, more on each floor. No other drops — no items, no eggs, no Ichor (except in the Sanguine Dimension, whose resource is Ichor). Your party still earns its ordinary experience.
  • What you win is yours at once. Each battle’s reward is banked the moment you win it.
  • Dying takes you out. A defeat ends the run; what you already won stays. It does not count as a death on the tower (your floor streak and the Abyss are untouched).
  • Leaving puts you back where you were. Leave the dimension any way you like and you return to the floor (or the Abyss) you entered from. Leaving ends the run — the key is spent. Closing the game is not leaving: you pick up where you stopped.
  • Every creature is marked. Creatures in a dimension wear its colours and carry its permanent buff, shown on their status bar like an über boss’s rule. It cannot be removed, stolen or shortened.

The floors

The five floors are the same in every dimension; only the buff, the colours and the resource change. Creature level and the strength of their gear rise together. The reward is what one dimension battle pays.

How the reward is set: ten times the expected yield of that resource from one ordinary battle on the highest wild floor at or below the creature level (floors 19, 39, 59, 79, 99) — the battle’s own Ichor plus the dismantle value of what drops, the same measure the Shadow Trader prices from. A full run of all 25 battles pays Scrap 725, Dust 165, Ichor 3,990, Essence 185 or Ink 165.

How hard is it?

  • Floors 1–2 fall to any party around level 40 and up.
  • Floor 3 is the test for a level-60 party.
  • Floor 5 is meant to be challenging but doable for a well-equipped party of level 90 and up: about two battles in three won. The Swollen Dimension is the hardest — double HP on everything.
  • Your party starts every battle at full health.
