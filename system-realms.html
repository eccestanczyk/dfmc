DEATH & REALMS

Other Systems › Death & Realms

Herumon Tower has one realm. Every account holds one character, one save, one leaderboard presence. Death costs the win streak and nothing else — characters, creatures and items persist forever. (Autoplay can be set to step down one floor after a defeat — On Death: Go Down, its default — but that is your own setting, not a penalty; see Autoplay.)

One game mode

There is one game mode. Hardcore, the seasonal ladders and the crypt bag (the Bequest inbox) are no longer in the game.

The floor-90 den, The Undying Crypt (Varkolan, the Undying King, and its Void Apex twin on floor 118), is a place in the tower and has nothing to do with the old crypt bag.
