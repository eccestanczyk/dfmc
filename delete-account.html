How to delete your Herumon Tower account and everything it holds, from inside the game or from any browser. This page is the deletion link the app stores ask for. The full list of what is deleted and what is kept is in the Privacy Policy, §6 Deleting your account.

From a browser

The button opens the game and goes straight to the deletion dialog. If you are not signed in, sign in through Steam first, then press the button again.

DELETE MY ACCOUNT

From inside the game

  1. Open Settings (the cogwheel), then Account & Data.
  2. Press Delete Account. The first dialog lists what will be deleted. Press CONTINUE.
  3. Type DELETE and press DELETE ACCOUNT.

What happens next

  • You are signed out on every device at once, and the deletion is scheduled for 7 days later.
  • If you sign in during those 7 days, the game shows Deletion scheduled on <date>. CANCEL DELETION keeps the account exactly as it was. CONTINUE signs you out again, and the deletion stays scheduled.
  • After the 7 days, the account is deleted within a day and cannot be recovered. Wraithgold you bought is deleted with it and is not refunded.
  • If you sign in with the same Steam account afterwards, you start a brand-new account.

If you cannot sign in, write to [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL] with your Steam profile link and we delete the account for you.
