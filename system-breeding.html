BREEDING

Other Systems › Breeding

Breeding is a continuous pen, not a one-off trade. Put a compatible pair in a pen and it starts on its own: it eats Ichor minute by minute and fills a progress bar. When the bar fills, an egg drops into your soul bag and the pen keeps going. Breeding opens at floor 25. Ichor is the limit on how fast it runs; the Shadow Trader also takes it in trade.

The pen grid

Six pens, shown as a 2×3 grid. Pen 1 is free. Pens 2–3 are bought with Ichor; pens 4–6 are premium, 5 / 10 / 15 Wraithgold. They unlock in order. A pen is bought once: a second click or a reload never charges you twice, and a bought pen stays bought.

Extra pens buy parallelism: several different pairs, types, and grade-ladders running at once, and no idle time while you swap partners. A player with six pens and a player with two pens hatch the same number of Void eggs per hour; the six-pen player is also climbing five other lines while they do it.

What decides an egg’s grade

The parents’ LEVEL does not matter. Their GRADE does, and nothing else does. A level 100 Worn pair and a level 1 Worn pair lay exactly the same eggs.

  1. The two parents’ grades make the pair grade: their average, rounded down (see Pair grade below). An Epic × Worn pair is floor((5+1)/2) = 3 — an Uncommon pen. A mismatched pair always averages down, so two parents of the same grade is the efficient pairing.
  2. The pair grade picks one row of the egg odds. The egg’s grade is then random within that row — the row sets the odds, not the result. A Void pen lays a Void egg 35.7% of the time and can still lay a Worn one 0.1% of the time.

The pair grade also sets the wait and the Ichor cost (Rates below). The egg’s grade and its line are rolled when it is laid, and a reload cannot re-roll them. Full odds under Egg grade output.

Choosing a pair

The pen has two sockets, ♂ and ♀. Clicking a socket opens a full-screen roster picker — it leaves the pen grid rather than crowding it, so the 2×3 grid stays intact.

Type scarcity is the real gate. Across the roster of 312, the median creature has about 45 type-compatible species and the tightest has 11. The four three-creature types — Celestial, Demon, Chimera, Aberration — are a single line each, so they can only breed within their own line. Breeding rewards a broad collection, not a strong one.

What the egg contains

The egg carries one of the two parents' lines, rolled 50/50 at the moment it is laid — never a third species. It hatches that line's Stage 1, like every other egg in the game. The shared type is the gate on which pairs may breed; it does not decide what comes out, so pairing a Thornback with a Dusthound returns a Bristle or a Whelp and nothing else. Its grade is rolled separately, from the pair grade (above).

The line is fixed when the egg is laid, not when it hatches, so an egg sitting in the soul bag already knows what it is.

Pair grade

Every rate in the system keys off one number: pair grade P.

P = floor( (grade of parent A + grade of parent B) / 2 )

Grades count Worn 1, Common 2, Uncommon 3, Rare 4, Epic 5, Legendary 6, Abyssal 7, Divine 8, Primordial 9, Void 10. Mismatched parents are punished by the rounding: a Void paired with a Rare breeds as Abyssal (P = 7), not as Void. Climbing the grade ladder means lifting both parents.

The loop

StateBehaviour
Pair placedA pen holding a compatible pair is breeding — there is no start button and no toggle. The pen spends its pair grade’s Ichor per minute and fills its meter.
Bar fullThe pen shows LAYING while the egg is delivered, then the egg goes to your soul bag, the bar resets to zero, and the pen continues automatically as long as Ichor and bag space remain. While LAYING it spends nothing; if the game cannot reach the server, the egg is owed, not lost, and lands once it can.
Soul bag fullThe pen freezes and shows SOUL BAG FULL. Nothing is consumed and no egg is lost — it lays as soon as a slot is free.
Out of IchorThe pen pauses (NO ICHOR) without consuming anything and resumes the moment Ichor arrives. With only part of a minute’s Ichor it crawls along at what it can pay for (LOW ICHOR).
Removing a parentResets that pen's meter to zero. This is what stops a player filling a bar on a cheap Worn pair and swapping in two Voids at 99%.
ClockSession time only, and session means the game is open — not that it is in front of you. A pen advances on real elapsed time whenever the game is loaded, including while the window sits unfocused or behind another tab. It does not advance while the game is closed: no offline progress, ever.

How the meter advances

  • The meter follows real elapsed time, not frames drawn. A browser that slows a background tab credits the whole gap on its next step, so the pen finishes at the same moment either way.
  • One pen-minute is one real minute. An egg is never delivered before its full wait has passed on the server’s clock, so changing your device’s clock does not speed a pen up.

Ichor income

Ichor comes from combat, and scales with what you killed — level and rarity both. Per defeated enemy:

Ichor = round( 2 × (1 + enemyLevel / 100) × RarityMult )

RarityMult:   C 1.0   UC 1.1   R 1.2   SR 1.35   SSR 1.5

Enemy level counts up to 100, so the Void Apex pays the floor-100 rate.

No breed-and-dismantle loop. An egg’s expected dismantle value is always well below what the pen spent making it: about 39% of the cost on a Worn pair, under 30% from Common up, and under 10% from Abyssal up. Breeding for Ichor is always a loss; dismantling is a refund on a bad roll, never an engine.

Egg grade output

The pair grade P picks a row of the same odds table that later rolls the hatchling’s grade from the egg’s grade (see Eggs). Two Void parents roll a Void egg 35.7% of the time (20% Primordial, 9% Divine, 10% Abyssal, 19% Legendary, and a tail below), and that Void egg then hatches a Void creature 35.7% of the time: ~13% Void end-to-end, with a real chance of falling short at either step.

Throughput ceiling

Ichor income — not pen count — is the hard cap on egg production.

A single Void pen alone (1,560 Ichor/h) eats about nine-tenths of a maxed player's entire kill income; a second Void pen is already past it. That is the intended shape: the top of the ladder is slow by construction, and no amount of pens buys past it.

Adam and Eve — the soul-bag pair keeper

An option in the soul-bag dismantle menu (soul bag only: it needs lines and genders). It keeps the highest-grade MALE and the highest-grade FEMALE of every advancement line and dismantles the rest. Two keepers per line, not one — a breeding pair needs both, which is the whole point of the name.

What it never dismantles:

  • a creature whose gender is not known — it is kept;
  • a creature whose line cannot be worked out;
  • a creature above level 1;
  • mutants;
  • a creature of a grade you have switched off in the window. Abyssal and up start switched off; the switches last for that one sweep. A switched-off creature can still be its line’s keeper;
  • class souls, eggs, and everything ordinary bulk dismantle already leaves alone. Creatures in pens are not in the bag, so they are never touched.

Ties are broken by level, then by stage, and the confirmation window says so.

The confirmation window explains what it does: it names the rule, how many survive across how many lines, how many are being dismantled, and every creature it is leaving alone and why.
