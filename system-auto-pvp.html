AUTO PVP

Other Systems › Auto PVP

Asynchronous ranked combat. You attack a snapshot of another player's team — they are not online, they do not need to be, and their side is piloted by their own autoplay settings. Opens at floor 15. 10 attacks a day, matches decided by the server, standings cut every week for pay — there are no seasons, and nothing ever resets your rating. Competitive PvP (live, double-blind, both players present) lives on its own page.

The loop

StepWhat happens
QueueSpends one attack and starts a match. The server picks the defender from every player past the Auto PvP unlock floor with a saved defense whose power is within ×1.25 of yours (widening to ×1.5 and ×2 when few are in range), or a TOWER ECHO house team built for your best floor when nobody is. An echo fight moves no rating and does not count toward the week’s pay. Both teams and the battle seed are fixed at that moment, and a 20-minute clock starts.
FightManual or autoplay, exactly like any other battle. The defense is piloted by the defender’s own autoplay settings. Every unit on both sides fights on ×1.5 its max HP, and Competitive’s damage ramp applies.
FinishThe server re-runs the whole fight from the fixed seed and teams and settles it; both ratings move. Every move starts the fight ready. A result the server’s replay does not confirm is void: a roster that does not match the teams fixed at the queue, an illegal action, or a different winner — the attack stays spent, neither rating moves, the match counts for nobody, and it is flagged for review.
AbandonAn Auto PvP fight does not resume after a reload or crash. A match left unfinished past 20 minutes settles as a defense win. The attack is not refunded — walking away from a bad matchup is not free.

Attacks

  • 10 per day, refilling to that number at the daily reset (00:00 UTC). Nothing banks: unspent attacks do not carry over.
  • At most two attacks on the same defender per day, so no one can be farmed and no one can farm one soft target.
  • The premium shop sells 10 more for the current day, 4 Wraithgold. They stack above the daily ten and expire at the same reset.
  • You never choose your opponent. The server picks from players near your hidden defense rating, so matches stay competitive as you climb.

The snapshot

Your defense is a copy of your team taken once a day — the team in your last save, with its gear, levels and moves frozen as they were. Your attack is the team you have equipped when you queue: the game saves before it queues, and the server reads your attacking team from that save and fixes it for the match. Both sides of every match are a fixed, knowable thing.

  • Your defense fights while you are offline. It is piloted by your autoplay settings — ultimate timing, targeting, buff and debuff priority — so those settings are your defensive strategy, not just a convenience.
  • Re-gearing shows up on defense at the next daily reset — the snapshot is a day's photograph, not a live mirror — and on attack at once, from your next queue.
  • An account that has never saved has no team slice: it cannot be attacked, and it cannot attack (“NO TEAM SAVED YET”).

Rating

  • Glicko-2, seeded at 1500. Every player carries two ratings: a visible attack rating and a hidden defense rating.
  • Attacking moves your attack rating; being attacked moves only your defense rating. A bad week of defenses cannot drag your visible rank down.
  • The hidden rating is what matchmaking steers by. It is never shown, to you or to anyone else.
  • The ladder shows what each player last won with — their class and the two creatures beside it.

Weeks, pay and inactivity

There are no seasons. The ladder runs on weeks: the week closes on a fixed Sunday 00:00 UTC cutoff, the same clock as the daily reset, the standings are cut, the top ten are paid, and every rating carries on unchanged — nothing resets.

  • Pay by rank: rank 1 gets incense and bait for 30 days plus the week’s medal; ranks 2–3 incense and bait for 30 days; ranks 4–10 incense for 30 days. The items arrive as timed boons on the account.
  • You are paid only if you played that week: at least one attack of yours that settled and was not voided. Defending does not count, and neither does a fight against a Tower Echo.
  • A thin week pays half. A week in which fewer than 10 players attacked pays 50% of every reward’s days and no medal.
  • Inactivity hides you: after 7 days without a settled attack you leave the Auto PvP board. From idle week 2 each full idle week takes 5% off your rating, down to the starting 1500 and never below it. Your next attack starts from the decayed rating and puts you back on the board.

Challenge by code

From the arena header, CHALLENGE lets you hand one player a code. If they are waiting for you live, the two of you get a Competitive match; if they are not, entering their code is one Auto PvP attack on their defense — same ticket, same limit of two attacks per defender per day, the same server replay and settle. Fair play

Every result is decided by the server, not reported by the client. The fight you watch and the fight the server verifies run the same code over the same bytes — one battle engine, shipped to both sides.

  • The defense's moves are never accepted from an attacker's client: the server derives them from the pinned settings, so a weakened defense is a mismatch, not an advantage.
  • The server builds both teams. Every stat, the gear package and every move of both sides is derived on the server from the saved roster — class soul and its level, equipped gear, sockets and souls, the two equipped creatures and their levels — against the codex tables, with the same code the game shows you. Nothing the client writes about a team's numbers is read.
  • Submitted teams are checked field by field against the snapshot pinned at mint — stats, gear, class, and every move including its effects.
  • A result that fails either check is recorded and investigated rather than silently accepted.
