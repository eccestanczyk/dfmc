AUTO PVP

Other Systems › Auto PVP

Asynchronous ranked combat. You attack a snapshot of another player's team — they are not online, they do not need to be, and their side is piloted by their own autoplay settings. Ten attacks a day, matches decided by the server, seasons closed weekly. Competitive PvP (live, double-blind, both players present) is unchanged and lives on its own page.

The loop

StepWhat happens
QueueSpends one attack and mints a match: the server picks the defender, pins the seed, pins both teams, and starts a 20-minute clock.
FightManual or autoplay, exactly like any other battle. The defense is piloted by the defender's own autoplay settings, and Competitive's damage ramp applies.
FinishThe server re-simulates the whole fight from the pinned seed and teams. Agreement settles the match; both ratings move. Cooldowns are the server’s: every move starts the fight ready, the re-sim tracks each one itself, and a submitted round that fires a move still cooling is flagged, never settled. A claim the server disproves is void: a roster that does not match the teams pinned at the queue, an illegal act in the replay, or a re-sim that reaches another winner — the ticket stays spent, neither rating moves, the match counts for nobody, and a report is filed for D.
AbandonA match left unfinished past 20 minutes settles as a defense win. The attack is not refunded — walking away from a bad matchup is not free.

Attacks

  • Ten per day, refilling to ten at the daily reset (00:00 UTC). Nothing banks: unspent attacks do not carry over.
  • At most two attacks on the same defender per day, so no one can be farmed and no one can farm one soft target.
  • The premium shop sells ten more for the current day, 4 Wraithgold. They stack above the daily ten and expire at the same reset.
  • You never choose your opponent. The server picks from players near your hidden defense rating, so matches stay competitive as you climb.

The snapshot

Your defense is a copy of your team taken by the daily fold — the same team you last saved, with its gear, levels and moves frozen as they were. Your attack is the team you have equipped when you queue (D ruling 2026-08-25: “The team you take to async pvp needs to be the one you currently have equipped. The daily snapshot is for the DEFENDING team”). The client saves before it queues, and the server reads the attacking team from that save and pins it for the match — the client never names its own team — so both sides of every match are still a fixed, knowable thing.

  • Your defense fights while you are offline. It is piloted by your autoplay settings — ultimate timing, targeting, buff and debuff priority — so those settings are your defensive strategy, not just a convenience.
  • Re-gearing shows up on defense at the next daily reset — the snapshot is a day's photograph, not a live mirror — and on attack at once, from your next queue. (Until 2026-08-25 both sides used the daily snapshot; report #510.)
  • An account that has never saved has no team slice: it cannot be attacked, and it cannot attack (“NO TEAM SAVED YET”).

Rating

  • Glicko-2, seeded at 1500. Every player carries two ratings: a visible attack rating and a hidden defense rating.
  • Attacking moves your attack rating; being attacked moves only your defense rating. A bad week of defenses cannot drag your visible rank down.
  • The hidden rating is what matchmaking steers by. It is never shown, to you or to anyone else.
  • The ladder shows what each player last won with — their class and the two creatures beside it.

Seasons

  • Seasons close on a fixed Sunday 00:00 UTC cutoff, the same clock as the daily reset.
  • Ratings carry over. Closing a season settles the standings and pays the top ten.
  • Rewards are items, by rank: top 1, top 3, top 10. The current placeholders are incense and bait for the top three and incense through rank ten. A week’s reward requires at least one match you attacked in that week — no match, no reward for that week. Defending does not count, and neither does a voided match.

Fair play

Every result is decided by the server, not reported by the client. The fight you watch and the fight the server verifies run the same code over the same bytes — one battle engine, shipped to both sides.

  • The defense's moves are never accepted from an attacker's client: the server derives them from the pinned settings, so a weakened defense is a mismatch, not an advantage.
  • The server builds both teams. Every stat, the gear package and every move of both sides is derived on the server from the saved roster — class soul and its level, equipped gear, sockets and souls, the two equipped creatures and their levels — against the codex tables, with the same code the game shows you. Nothing the client writes about a team's numbers is read (D ruling 2026-10-01, Rulings board row teamstats).
  • Submitted teams are checked field by field against the snapshot pinned at mint — stats, gear, class, and every move including its effects.
  • A result that fails either check is recorded and investigated rather than silently accepted.

Defense log

The PVP screen reports what happened while you were away: how many times your defense was attacked, how many held, and who came at you with what. Controlled by Notifications › PvP Defense Results.

Changelog

  • 2026-10-01 — the server builds both teams from the saved roster (D ruling, Rulings board: row teamstats, “SERVER AUTHORITATIVE, EVERYTHING”). On dev; release needs the worker deploy. The pinned teams used to be a team summary the game client wrote into the save (stats, gear package, moves and their effects); the attacker's at the mint, the defender's at the daily fold. One edit of that summary before a save was a forged team the server then verified a fight against. Both are now built on the server from what the save holds — class, gear, souls, the equipped creatures and their XP — against the codex tables: the stat and gear math is the one the power board uses, and the move list and move effects are the game's own code, kept identical by a build check. Honest teams are unchanged, number for number; a forged summary changes nothing. Nothing changes for players; an older client keeps working. Defense teams pinned before the deploy are replaced at the next daily fold.
  • 2026-10-01 — a disproved result is void, and a week’s reward needs a match that week (D rulings, Rulings board: card apvp, row season). On dev; release needs the worker deploy. Void: since the 08-16 burn-in, a result the server’s check disproved was flagged and still paid as claimed: the attacker took the rating and the honest defender lost it. Every benign cause of a flag has since been fixed, so a flag now means a cheat or a client bug. Such a match is now refused: the ticket stays spent, no rating or win/loss record moves on either side, and the automatic report is still filed (one per reason per day, with the running count and the last match). The match-end note reads VOIDED instead of a victory. Season attendance: the reward used to require one settled match ever, attack or defense, so a top-ten account kept collecting every week after a single fight. A week’s reward now requires an attack you started in that week that closed as a real result (a void does not count); a missed week, including one closed late by the catch-up settle, pays only those who attacked in it.

  • 2026-09-30 — the finish door owns the starting board and ultimates are once per battle (project review F187, F188). On dev; LIVE on release since 2026-10-01 (0.88).. The re-sim used to take the attacker’s submitted starting board as-is — hit points, knock-outs, statuses and the fight-frozen Attack/Defense anchors were neither checked nor reset — so a claim with the defense at 1 HP, already knocked out, or an attacker at a hundred times his Attack re-simulated as a win and settled clean. Now every fight replays from the server’s own round 0: every unit at full HP, alive, no statuses, no ultimate spent, and an empty slot stays empty. The Attack/Defense anchors are re-derived from the pinned team slice with the client’s own formula and pinned (an attacker creature’s Defense may sit anywhere its own conditional Defense souls allow), and the gear package, seat, side and hero flag must match the slice — any mismatch fails the units pin. Ultimates: the engine now refuses a queued ultimate once that unit has spent it, as the class codex has always said (once per battle); the finish door strips any ultimate row from a creature (only the two heroes carry one, from the class token), and a replay that queues an ultimate on a spent or row-less unit ends there. Both are flagged exactly like a units-pin failure (paid during burn-in, reported) — the burn-in policy is unchanged. Honest play is unchanged: the client already starts every fight this way and only offers an ultimate while it is unspent. Gate: tools/probe_f187_apvp_round0.mjs 9/21 on the old door, 21/21 after; the F056 probe and the battle-module parity probe stay green.
  • 2026-09-29 — the finish door checks cooldowns (project review F056). On dev; LIVE on release since 2026-10-01 (0.88).. The shared battle engine never read a queued move’s cooldown — only the client’s buttons and autoplay brain honoured it — and the finish door replays the rounds the attacker submits, so a doctored claim that fired a cooldown-3 Nuke every round re-simulated as a win and settled. Now the re-sim ignores whatever cooldowns the claim carries, starts every move ready and tracks cooldowns from the engine’s own round tick, and the first round that queues a living attacker onto a move still cooling ends the replay: the match is flagged exactly like a roster that fails the units pin (paid during burn-in, reported). The engine itself now also refuses to fire a cooling ally move anywhere — the unit sits the round out — which changes nothing in honest play, because the client never queues one. The defender’s cooldowns are unchanged by this entry. Gate: tools/probe_f056_apvp_cd.mjs 4/10 on the old engine, 10/10 after; the battle-module parity probe stays green.
