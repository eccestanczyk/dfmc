EQUIPMENT, THE GEAR LOCK & AUTO-OPTIMIZE

Other Systems › Equipment & Auto-Optimize

Equipment can be changed at any time, including in the middle of a fight. What it cannot do is change a fight already in progress: every combat bonus is snapshotted when the battle starts and served from that snapshot until the battle ends.

The gear lock

  • All equipment-derived combat values — flat and percentage stats, souls, thorns, heal-received, debuff potency and duration, type and kind multipliers — come from gearBonus().
  • At the start of every battle the current bonus is computed and frozen. For the rest of the battle gearBonus() returns that frozen copy and ignores the live loadout entirely.
  • The freeze is taken at four entry points, each of which begins a battle: a wild encounter, a boss challenge, a re-entered fight from the victory screen, and a battle resumed after a page reload.
  • Creature and character unit stats (HP, attack, defence, speed, moves) are separate snapshots taken by initUnits() at the same moment, so they behave identically.
  • Out of battle the lock does not apply and the live loadout is authoritative immediately.

The lock binds the FIGHT, not the SHEET (D ruling 2026-08-02). Every display surface reads the live loadout: the character stat panel, POWER, and the creature cards all update the instant an item is swapped, mid-battle or not. Only the combat path sees the frozen copy. Before this the freeze applied to everything, so a player who swapped a weapon during a fight saw no number move anywhere on the equipment screen and had no way to tell the swap had taken. Display producers are wrapped in a helper that lifts the freeze for the duration of the read and restores it on the way out — including on a throw, so a render can never leak live gear into the fight in progress.

Why it is taken deliberately and not lazily. The freeze used to happen on whichever combat read called gearBonus() first. That was only safe while the equipment window and the optimizer both refused to run during a battle — once the battle gate came off, a player could change their loadout before any combat read and the NEW gear would have been frozen instead of the old, silently altering the fight in progress. The snapshot is now taken at battle entry, before anything can act.

Which stat an item grants

An item's stat comes from its own row in items.csv, never from the slot it sits in. itemStat() reads the Effect column and matches the authored grammar (+flat X for gear, tags and gems; increased % X for runes). The slot's default stat is only a fallback for an item with no effect row at all.

This matters most for accessories, which are the one category where the family decides the stat:

FamilyEffectGrants
Signet+Attack (implicit, 6×T×GF, Local)Attack
Relic+HP (implicit, 9×T×GF, Local)HP

Both go in the same two Accessory slots, whose slot-level default is Attack — so any surface that labels off the slot instead of the item will call a Relic an Attack item while showing its correct HP number. Weapons and armour hide this class of bug, because there the slot default happens to match the item.

Auto-optimize

  • Searches the equipped items plus everything in the bag and proposes the loadout with the highest character Power, reshuffling gems and runes globally across the result. Scrolls never move.
  • It touches gear slots only. The two creature slots (Monster I and II) are outside its order and are never changed by it — see The Active Party.
  • It runs during a battle (D ruling 2026-08-02). The change lands immediately and, because of the lock above, takes effect from the next battle.
  • The preview is re-derived and compared before it is applied; if anything moved in between it is refused with “Things changed — run it again” rather than applied against a stale plan.
  • If the resulting loadout needs more bag room than the account has, it is blocked before anything moves.

Scoring must bypass the lock. The optimizer measures a candidate by swapping it into state and asking the game for its own Power number. During a battle gearBonus() would answer with the frozen snapshot for every candidate alike, so every option scored identically and the optimizer reported “already optimal” whatever was in the bag. The measurement therefore lifts the freeze for the duration of the swap and restores it afterwards. This was found by probe the moment the battle gate was removed — the gate had been hiding it.

Client: gearBonus(), freezeGear(), optBuildPlan(), optScore(), optApply() in play/app.js

The soul picker

Ordered grade > level > line. The list built its cards straight off BAG.concat(bagAdd) — insertion order — so a Divine sat wherever it happened to have been hatched and the player had to hunt for it. It now sorts by GRADE_ORDER descending, then level descending, then line, then name.

  • Line is the third key, not the first. Grade decides the slot, level decides between equals, and the line only groups what is otherwise identical. Name breaks the final tie so the order cannot shuffle between re-renders on equal creatures.
  • Same comparator shape as the breeding picker and the inventory, which is the point: three lists that order creatures should not each invent their own order. The breeding selector had this exact defect on 2026-08-05.
  • BAG is copied before sorting. It is not React state, and the bag's own order is its identity — sorting it in place would reorder the inventory as a side effect of opening a picker.
  • Hovering a candidate shows its move summary. The rows always set eqHMv; eqHoverOn carried !s.eqPick, so the summary was suppressed precisely while a picker was open — the one screen where a player most needs it was the one screen that refused it. Fixed 2026-08-05.
  • The containment rule is not abandoned, only excepted. The original rule — “never paints over a picker or a confirm” — still holds for sock picks and confirms. Over a SOUL picker the panel is allowed, and two things make that safe rather than a straight reversal: it is pinned to top:82% so it does not sit on the list being hovered, and it is pointer-events:none so it cannot steal the mouse off a row. Without the second, hovering would drop the hover the instant the panel appeared, and flicker.
  • Each row states the soul's strength as the live stage × grade figure. It previously read “60% strength” from the retired stage ladder — a number that stopped existing when the surface was rebased to ×1.00–×2.50 earlier the same day. Anything quoting a multiplier reads it from the same place the maths does.

Probe: tools/probe_soulhover.py (BEFORE 4/4, AFTER 5/5). It dispatches a real mouseover on the row, because the renderer strips sc-camel-on-mouse-enter from the DOM and there is no attribute to select on. LIVE on release since 2026-08-05 (0.32).

The soul readout

Each of the eight gear slots shows a readout of what its soul grants — the move for an ability slot, the passive text for an armour slot. Since 2026-08-05 that panel also reports the slotted creature, not only its effect:

  • An XP bar fills the bottom edge of the readout, and the creature’s current level sits bottom-right (26px stage, 9.34 CSS px on a phone).
  • These are live. A socketed soul is a creature and it levels — battleRewards banks XP into soulOv[gi].src — so the bar is read through monXpOf/xpLevel, the same pair the creature sheet uses. The bar and the sheet cannot disagree, and neither is a snapshot taken when the creature was consumed.
  • Gender appears on the soul tile and on the party creature card. Resolved with monGenderKnown, never monGender: the latter defaults to M so breeding always has a side to compare, and printing that default as fact is exactly what the UI must not do. A creature with no stamped gender shows no glyph.

Probe: tools/probe_eqsoulxp.py (BEFORE 4/4, AFTER 6/6). The party card carries its own level label at 15px stage — 5.39 CSS px, still sub-legible and not yet raised; see The Stage. LIVE on release since 2026-08-05 (0.32).

The item details window

Clicking an item in the inventory opens its details. What that window offers is decided by the item's category.

ActionShown for
DISMANTLEWeapon, Armour, Accessory, Gem, Rune, Scroll, Tag, Key — and only when dismYield actually prices the item. Tag and Key were added 2026-08-05: both were already priced (Key on the tier ladder, Tag by grade and tier) and the button was hidden by a category whitelist alone. Bulk sweep still never touches a Key — a key is the gate on a boss fight, so it goes one at a time, as a deliberate act.
GO TO FLOOR nKeys only, and only when the floor is unlocked (maxFloor ≥ keyFloor − 1). When the floor is locked the button is absent, not present and refusing.
EQUIP / UPGRADEUnchanged — gear, tags and slottables route to the Equipment window; creatures never do.

A tag's SLOT row reads “Tag”. The layout names the two creature tag sockets Tag A and Tag B so it can tell them apart, and equipItem writes slot:EQUIP[i].slot onto a tag it hands back to the bag. An unequipped tag therefore carried Tag B into its details and read as though it only fitted that socket. It fits either. Fresh drops never showed this, which is why it survived.

The boss route does not chain across a transit. enterBossRoom() derives its target from bossFloor(), which reads the current floor, so it can only be called once the player is already in that boss's decade. If they are, the boss room opens directly; otherwise they are taken to the boss's approach floor and enter by the normal door. Travel is asynchronous and this area produced the orphaned-veil softlock on 2026-08-04, so nothing is sequenced across it. Probe: tools/probe_itemdet.py. LIVE on release since 2026-08-05 (0.32).

Soul strength — stage × grade

A gear soul’s output (passive magnitude, and granted move power on the four ability slots) is scaled by two multipliers that multiply together. They are set as one curve, because neither is meaningful alone.

LeverRangeWhat it costs
Source creature’s stageS1 ×1.00 · S2 ×1.12 · S3 ×1.25 Raised by advancing the creature — levels, not resources.
Source creature’s quality gradeWorn ×1.00 → Void ×2.00, nine equal steps (1 + (1/9) × (grade# − 1))Inherited from the egg. Creatures are not upgraded with resources — they are bred and hatched, so this lever is raised by breeding for a better egg, and above Legendary it is a breeding pursuit outright. See Breeding.

The combined surface therefore runs ×1.00 at S1/Worn to ×2.50 at S3/Void. Nothing is ever below ×1.00 — a soul is never a penalty, only a bonus that grows.

Rebased 2026-08-05 (D). It previously ran ×0.60 (S1/Worn) to ×1.45 (S3/Void), so most souls sat below 1 and read as a penalty. Span is close to unchanged (2.42× → 2.50×) — a rebase, not an inflation; modelled avatar power at the ceiling +7.7%. The levers also swapped weight: stage used to carry 1.67× and grade only 1.45×, which put the wider range on the lever that costs least. Grade now carries 2.00× — a Void creature is a breeding outcome, and consuming one for its soul should read as a real decision. Recalculated with tools/statmodel.py against the three anchor creatures, never hand-modelled.

Not this lever, but recorded while looking: the CLASS-soul upgrade cost does not reconcile. Class souls (CLS-*) do have a resource ladder and it keeps its own grade field; creature souls never did. Three sources disagree on what that ladder costs and none can be quoted until D rules: Upgrading states mult × 5 units per attempt, but soul_grading.csv’s last row is 1200 Scrap = 80 units at the stated 1 unit = 15 Scrap and no SoulMult × 5 reaches 80; and the CSV contradicts that rate internally, every row’s Scrap column implying twice the units its Dust/Essence/Ink columns do.

Known, and not a soul problem. Solving for the combined multiplier that would put a maxed L100 avatar on the median maxed-SSR-creature power does not converge — at ×3.00 the avatar is still 32.8% short, and atk never moves at all, because LOCAL souls only multiply Defence on three armour slots. The avatar/creature parity target cannot be reached through the soul ladder and needs its own lever.
