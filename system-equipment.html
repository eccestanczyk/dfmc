EQUIPMENT, THE GEAR LOCK & AUTO-OPTIMIZE

Other Systems › Equipment & Auto-Optimize

Equipment can be changed at any time, including in the middle of a fight. What it cannot do is change a fight already in progress: a battle keeps the loadout it started with until it ends.

The equipment screen at a glance

  • Eight gear slots on the character — Main Hand, Off Hand, two Accessories, Head, Chest, Gloves, Feet — and two creature slots (Monster I and II), each creature wearing up to two Tags. The creature slots belong to The Active Party; this page is about the gear.
  • What a piece pays is a base number × its tier (I–X) × its grade factor (Worn ×1.00 … Void ×2.08, Grade). Weapons pay Attack (a Warrior or Professor off hand pays Defense), armour pays Defense, and accessories pay by family (a Signet is Attack, a Relic is HP) — table below.
  • Sockets: gems (flat), runes (percentage) and one enchant scroll per socket, as many of each as the piece’s grade allows — Sockets.
  • Souls: every gear slot also seats a creature soul. In the four hand and accessory slots the soul lends one of its line’s moves; in the four armour slots it lends a passive. The character’s own class soul sets the class. A soul’s strength is its stage × its grade (table below); a Team passive reaches the character and both monsters, a Personal one the character only, a Local one the piece it sits on. Souls are bred, graded and devoured on their own pages (Breeding, Upgrading, Devour).
  • Level requirement: a piece or stone of tier T needs level (T−1)×10 (T1 = 1) — your account level on character gear, the creature’s level on a tag. The picker will not equip an over-level piece; one already worn above its level stays on but counts for nothing until you reach the level.
  • POWER is Attack + Defense + HP/5 over the character and both companions, every soul counted by what it does on your build; it is the figure the Character Power board ranks.
  • AUTO-OPTIMIZE (under CLASSES in the player panel) proposes the strongest loadout from what you wear and what is in your bag, previews the moves it would make, and reshuffles gems and runes across the result; scrolls never move. BUILDS, beside it, saves and restores up to five loadouts per class line on the server. DISMANTLE and FILTERS are menu plates on the inventory side.
  • Changing gear never changes a fight in progress: the battle keeps the loadout it started with, and your new gear counts from the next one (the gear lock, below).

The gear lock

  • When a battle starts, everything your equipment adds to that fight is fixed for the rest of it: flat and percentage stats, souls, thorns, healing received, debuff strength and duration, type and kind bonuses. Your character’s and creatures’ own stats and moves are fixed at the same moment.
  • This holds for every way a battle starts: a wild encounter, a boss challenge, a fight re-entered from the victory screen, and a battle resumed after a page reload.
  • You can still change gear mid-battle. The change lands at once and counts from the next battle.
  • The screen always shows your current loadout: the stat panel, POWER and the creature cards update the moment you swap a piece, in battle or not. Only the fight in progress keeps the old numbers.
  • Out of battle there is no lock: a change counts immediately.

Auto-optimize

  • Looks at what you wear plus everything in your bag and proposes the strongest loadout for your Optimize For setting (in Settings: Overall Power, ATK, DEF or HP; Overall Power by default). Gems and runes are reshuffled across the whole result.
  • It never moves creatures, souls or scrolls. The two creature slots (Monster I and II) are outside it — see The Active Party.
  • A plan never lowers the number you optimize for; when that number would stay the same, it applies only if Overall Power rises. If nothing would help, it says so and names what it looked at.
  • It shows a preview first. If anything changed between the preview and your confirm, nothing is applied: THINGS CHANGED — RUN IT AGAIN.
  • If the new loadout needs more bag room than you have, it is blocked before anything moves.
  • If applying a plan would lose or duplicate any item, the whole change is cancelled: OPTIMIZE CANCELLED — NOTHING WAS CHANGED.
  • It never proposes a piece or stone above its level requirement.
  • It works during a battle. The change lands at once and counts from the next battle (the gear lock above).
A low-tier piece can beat a high-tier one. Socket count comes from grade, not tier (Void 3 gems + 3 runes, Rare 1 + 1), so a T4 Void helmet with six stones can outscore a T10 Rare helmet with two. The optimizer scores each piece’s real stat contribution, and the tier is already inside that number.

What a soul’s text shows

  • The passive list on the equipment screen and the slot’s hover tip quote the effective number, stage × grade already applied — PERSONAL: +6.2% Attack, not the base +4%.
  • Only the size of the effect scales. A condition never does: +5% Defense while above 50% HP still fires above 50% HP.
  • The item details window shows the working: ×N STRENGTH (Sstage × grade) and raw → effective for every slot in the line.
  • The creature sheet’s WHEN SLOTTED IN PASSIVE ARMOUR panel quotes the passive at that creature’s current stage.

The soul readout

  • Each of the eight gear slots shows what its soul grants: the move on a hand or accessory slot, the passive text on an armour slot.
  • It also shows the socketed creature: an XP bar along the bottom edge and its level at bottom-right. A socketed soul is still a creature and keeps earning XP from your battles; the bar is live and matches the creature sheet.
  • Gender shows on the soul tile and on the party creature card. A creature with no recorded gender shows no glyph.

Tier plaques

  • Every item with a tier shows it as a Roman numeral on a small plate in the bottom-right corner, bordered in the item’s grade colour: inventory tiles, equipment slot rows, creature tag rows, the equip picker and the socket picker. Victory reward rows show it at the far right of the row.
  • Creatures, eggs, currency and empty slots have no tier and show no plate. Creatures and class souls show their level in that corner instead.

Level requirements

Every equippable piece of tier T needs level (T−1)×10: T1 = 1, T2 = 10, … T9 = 80, T10 = 90 — gear, tags, gems, runes and scrolls alike. The level tested depends on the slot:

SlotLevel tested
Character gear (both hands, both accessories, four armour)your account level (the number on the HUD)
Tag A / Tag B of Monster IMonster I’s level; an empty creature slot is not gated
Tag A / Tag B of Monster IIMonster II’s level; an empty creature slot is not gated

  • Equip picker: an over-level piece is listed greyed with LV n across the card; clicking it says LEVEL n REQUIRED (tags: CREATURE LEVEL n REQUIRED) and equips nothing.
  • Socket picker: an over-level gem, rune or scroll is greyed the same way and refused before anything is spent.
  • Equipment screen: a piece worn above its level counts for nothing; its slot shows a red ILLEGAL / LV n block. An over-level stone or scroll shows a red ILLEGAL cell with a tip naming the level.
  • Auto-optimize never proposes an over-level piece or stone.
  • The Character Power board applies the same rule: an over-level piece is worth nothing there either.

Already worn above level: nothing is unequipped or moved for you. The piece stays, contributes nothing, and comes alive by itself the moment the wearer reaches the level.

POWER — how every soul counts

POWER is Attack + Defense + HP/5 summed over your character and both creatures. Every seated soul counts, valued on the unit it affects, as the stat it adds there, times how often it is active, and scaled by its stage × grade as in battle — so a Void soul always outscores the same soul at Worn. The Character Power board ranks the POWER the server computes from your saved game; your own POWER sheet previews it with the same formula.

  • Attack, Defense and HP souls count as the stat they add: a Personal soul on your character, a Team soul on every ally. A soul is worth what it does to your build — +10% HP counts for more on a high-HP character, +10% Attack for more on a high-Attack one.
  • Conditional souls count as the same stat times the share of the fight their condition is true, measured over seeded Auto PvP fights and kept between 0.25 and 0.75 (first turn of battle: 0.5). “While any ally is X” counts in full when an X is seated and not at all when none is; “vs a named enemy type” counts by that type’s share of the creature codex.
  • Utility souls count as the stat they stand in for, times the share of the fight they touch: damage dealt as Attack, damage taken and healing received as Defense + HP/5, thorns as Attack scaled by damage taken per damage dealt, DoT potency as Attack × the DoT share of your damage (about 0.20); a +1 turn counts as the effect it extends over the usual duration (3 turns for a DoT, 2.6 for a debuff, 2.5 for a buff). “Fungal allies deal +N%” and the like count only on a seated unit of that type.

Saved builds

A saved build is the server’s record of a loadout: which of your own pieces sits in each gear slot, each soul slot and each socket. Each class line has its own 5 build slots. The BUILDS button sits in the player panel directly under CLASSES, beside AUTO-OPTIMIZE.

  • Save (SAVE CURRENT / OVERWRITE) records what you wear now; the game saves first, so the build matches your saved game. An older piece with no id cannot be named and is left out (the answer says how many).
  • Apply moves your own pieces back into the saved places, through the same moves an equip, a socket or a soul seat makes. A piece you no longer hold — dismantled, devoured, in a pen, gone — is skipped and named; everything else still applies. A stone whose gear is gone is skipped with it and stays in your bag. Whatever a saved piece displaces goes to your bag; a leaving piece’s gems and runes go to the bag and its scroll stays on it, as when you unequip by hand. Applying a build you already wear changes nothing.
  • Rename and delete change only the record; your gear stays where it is. A build can only be applied, renamed or deleted by its own account, and only on its own class line. An account reset deletes its builds.
