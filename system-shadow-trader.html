THE SHADOW TRADER

Other Systems › The Shadow Trader

A weekend-only tab of the Premium Shop. Every week the trader takes every currency except Wraithgold and hands back one currency — a different one each week — at a rate that is computed from how long each currency takes to earn, never authored by hand. Status: designed 2026-08-25 (D, report #475); the rate model is built and gated; the shop tab and the trade door are being built. ON DEV when they land, release at D's word.

The rules (D, 2026-08-25)

RuleWhat it means
Weekends onlyThe tab exists on Saturday and Sunday in the game's day (UTC, the same clock as the daily reset). Monday to Friday the shop shows nothing of him — no greyed tab, no teaser. A trade posted at the stroke of Monday is refused.
One currency a weekEach week has one target: the currency he pays out. Week 0 (the week of Monday 2026-08-24) is Ichor, then Scrap, Dust, Essence, Ink, and round again. He takes every other currency — Scrap, Ichor, Dust, Essence, Ink — and never Wraithgold, in or out.
Rates from acquisition timeThe price of one unit of the week's currency, paid in another, is the ratio of how fast the two are earned, times the trader's cut. Earned means: what a battle yields in expectation, through the same drop and dismantle tables the game runs on. See the model below.
Self-adjustingThere is no rate table anywhere. The rate is derived at request time from drop_chances_by_floor.csv, drop_grade_weights.csv, drop_rates.csv, encounter_weights.csv, floors.csv, items.csv, dismantle.csv, breeding.csv and the Ichor-per-kill formula. Raise or lower any of them and the rates follow on the next request — a balance change can never leave the trader out of step.
One rate for everyoneD, 2026-08-25: “The shadow trader cannot have a dynamic per account rate. It needs to be the same for everyone, and based off endgame playtime.” Every rate is computed at one reference floor, TRADER_REFERENCE_FLOOR (the endgame), never at the account's own floor. The two candidate floors are tabled below; the constant holds 100 until D picks.
The cutTRADER_PREMIUM = 0.25: you pay time-parity × 1.25, i.e. you get back 80% of what the same time would have earned you directly. One constant, in src/trader.js; D's to tune.
Whole numbers, no remainderA trade is quoted as pay P of A, receive G of B with both whole. P is exactly what G costs (rounded up to the unit); a request too small to buy one unit is refused, never partially taken.
Server-authoritativeLike every premium purchase: the client asks, the server prices, debits, credits and answers with the new balances, idempotent on the client's key. The client never names a rate.

The model — what one battle yields at floor F

Battles per hour cancel out of every ratio, so “acquisition time” reduces to expected units per battle. One battle at floor F, as the worker runs it:

  • Three kills, each of a rarity drawn from encounter_weights.csv for F.
  • Ichor per kill = round(2 × (1 + F/20) × mult[rarity]) — the battle credit's own formula, mult C 1 / UC 1.5 / R 2.5 / SR 4 / SSR 7.
  • Three lanes per kill — gear, enhancement, egg — each landing with Chance_<rarity> from drop_chances_by_floor.csv.
  • A gear drop is uniform over the items.csv bases of the floor's tier among Weapon / Armour / Accessory / Tag, graded by the floor's drop_grade_weights band, and dismantles per dismantle.csv (Gear rows; a Tag also returns Essence).
  • An enhancement drop is an even Gem / Rune / Scroll split, graded one band behind (drop_rates.csv Enhancement_Grade_Rule), and dismantles to Dust / Essence / Ink.
  • An egg drop is graded by the band clamped at the wild-egg cap and dismantles to breeding.csv Egg_Dismantle_Ichor.
  • Not modelled, on purpose: bosses, first-win bonuses, keys, shop drops — they are not the steady state a rate should describe.

Then, for the week's target B and any other currency A: rate(A→B) = perBattle(A) ÷ perBattle(B) × (1 + premium), in units of A per one B.

An Ichor week at floor 100: 1,000 Scrap buys 8,940 Ichor; 10 Dust buys 658 Ichor (time parity would be 82 Ichor per Dust; 66 after the cut). A Dust week at floor 100: one Dust costs about 103 Ichor, or 9.2 Scrap.

A note the model surfaced (not changed): drop_rates.csv says each lane lands “exactly 10% per battle (3 kills)”, but the authored per-kill chances, mix-weighted on the encounter weights, come to ~6.7% per kill — about 19% per battle per lane, roughly twice the stated figure. The trader's ratios are unaffected (every drop-fed currency scales together), but the sentence and the table disagree; D's call which is right.
