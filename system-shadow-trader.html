THE SHADOW TRADER

Other Systems › The Shadow Trader

The SHADOW TRADER is a tab of the Premium Shop, beside PREMIUM, open every day once you reach floor 40. Each week he pays out one currency — a different one each week — and takes any other currency except Wraithgold in exchange. His prices are computed from how long each currency takes to earn at your best floor, plus his cut; nobody sets them by hand.

The rules

RuleWhat it means
Open all weekThe tab is there every day. What he pays out turns over every Monday at 00:00 UTC (the same clock as the daily reset), and the tab says what next week pays.
One currency a weekThe weeks run Ichor, Scrap, Dust, Essence, Ink, then round again. He takes every other currency — Scrap, Ichor, Dust, Essence, Ink — and never Wraithgold, in or out.
Rates from earning timeThe price of one unit of the week’s currency, paid in another, is the ratio of how fast the two are earned, times the trader’s cut. Earned means what a battle yields on average, through the same drop and dismantle tables the game runs on (the model below). There is no rate table: when the game’s drop or dismantle numbers change, the rates follow.
Your own best floorYour rates are computed at your best floor, the one you can actually farm. A boss floor prices at the wild floor below it. The panel head reads RATES FOLLOW YOUR BEST FLOOR (N). There is no weekly cap.
The cut25%: you pay time parity × 1.25, i.e. you get back 80% of what the same play time would have earned you directly.
Whole numbers, no remainderA trade is quoted as pay P of A, receive G of B with both whole. P is exactly what G costs (rounded up to the unit); a request too small to buy one unit is refused, never partly taken.
Checked before it happensEvery line is priced and checked against the wallet the server holds for you. A line you cannot cover is refused, with what you hold and what it needs. Income you have not saved yet is saved first, so a trade never loses it.
Cooldown30 seconds between trades.
The tabA slider per other currency, from 0 to what you held when the shop opened. Above each, the rate reads 1 : x at rest and y : x as the slider moves (y paid, x received). ACQUIRING <total> <currency> at the very top. CANCEL / CONFIRM at the bottom; CONFIRM opens a window listing PAY y A → GET x B per line and the total; CONFIRM TRADE makes every line in one trade. After a trade both buttons count down 30 s. The whole tab fits its panel without scrolling.

The model — what one battle yields at floor F

Battles per hour cancel out of every ratio, so “time to earn” reduces to expected units per battle. One battle at floor F:

  • Three kills, each of a rarity drawn from the floor’s encounter odds.
  • Ichor per kill = round(2 × (1 + min(100, F)/100) × mult[rarity]), with mult C 1 / UC 1.1 / R 1.2 / SR 1.35 / SSR 1.5 — the same Ichor a kill pays in battle.
  • Three drops per kill — gear, enhancement, egg — each landing at the floor’s drop chance for that rarity.
  • A gear drop is an even pick among the Weapon / Armour / Accessory / Tag pieces of the floor’s tier, graded by the floor’s grade odds, and counted as what it dismantles to (Scrap; a Tag also returns Essence).
  • An enhancement drop is an even Gem / Rune / Scroll split, graded one band behind, and dismantles to Dust / Essence / Ink.
  • An egg drop is graded by the floor’s band, capped at the wild-egg cap, and dismantles to Ichor.
  • Not counted, on purpose: bosses, first-win bonuses, keys, shop drops — they are not the steady state a rate should describe.

Then, for the week’s currency B and any other currency A: rate(A→B) = perBattle(A) ÷ perBattle(B) × 1.25, in units of A per one B.
