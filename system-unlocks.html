Hub › Other Systems › System Unlocks

Systems open one at a time as you climb. Each rung of the ladder is a floor: win on it and the system opens, with one arrow pointing at its control until you click it once. No rung is bought: climbing alone opens the ladder.

The ladder

Single hatch and single dismantle are open from floor 1, and so is the soul bag window, so eggs can hatch from the start.

The opening and the arrows

  • The opening. Your first fight walks you through starting a fight, choosing a move, picking a target and locking in. The first floors then hand you what each lesson needs: a weapon for your class on floor 1, Scrap for one weapon upgrade on floor 2, a gem to socket on floor 3, an egg to hatch on floor 4; on floor 5 an arrow shows you how to dismantle a piece.
  • Autoplay opens when your first battle ends, won or lost — the one rung that is not a floor.
  • Every unlock is announced. A card names the system that just opened; its GO button takes you to the window that holds it.
  • One arrow per rung. When a system opens, an arrow with its label points at the control, follows it across windows, and goes away the first time you click that control. It never blocks play.
  • A locked control is shown locked, with the floor that opens it, so you know what is coming.
  • The class soul is yours from creation. Its UPGRADE opens with the Souls rung at floor 12.
  • Breeding pens open with Ichor or Wraithgold, not floors, once breeding itself is open at 25 (see Breeding).
