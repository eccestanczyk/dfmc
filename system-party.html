THE ACTIVE PARTY

Other Systems › The Active Party

A battle team is the character plus two creatures: a0 is the avatar, a1 and a2 are the two creature slots. The creature slots ARE equipment slots — Monster I and Monster II, equipOv[8] and equipOv[9] — and the equipment window is the only place the team is set.

Swap-only (D ruling 2026-08-02)

  • A creature slot can be REPLACED by picking another creature from the soul bag. The outgoing creature returns to the bag carrying its own record: uid, XP, grade, mutant flag and origin all ride with it.
  • A creature slot can NEVER be emptied. Clicking the equipped creature to unequip it is refused with “Can’t unequip the active party, only replace”. Gear slots are unaffected and still unequip normally.
  • The rule exists because an empty party slot is a state nothing downstream handles well — see below.

An empty slot is EMPTY

Saves written before the swap-only rule can still hold an empty creature slot (equipOv[9] = {name:''}), so the empty case must still behave. It resolves to a unit with no HP, no moves and no species, flagged empty:1:

  • ORDER/aliveOrder() filter on hp > 0, so an empty slot never takes a turn and can never be targeted.
  • The reward loop skips it explicitly (un.empty), so it earns no XP and puts no row on the victory screen.
  • It is not a defeat condition: alliesAlive is satisfied by the avatar.

The phantom starter (fixed 2026-08-02)

Reported by D: “I don't have any mon equipped on the second slot, but one still appears in battle and gets experience. It's a Taproot I don't even own.”

initUnits() builds the two creature slots with mkMon(gi, id, fb), and fb was the hardcoded Scoria and Taproot starters — dev-mock survivors from the pre-release build, still carrying sub:'Starter', SSR rarity and a YGGDRASIL/MAGMACRAWLER species the account had never owned. An empty slot returned that fallback, so the phantom fought, could be targeted, and was credited XP — and the XP was written back into the nameless equipOv record, where it accumulated invisibly. Reproduced on the reporting account: slot 9 held {name:'', xp:2431} and produced a Lv 1 Taproot that earned +47 XP in a single battle.

The general shape, worth remembering: a fallback that substitutes real game content for missing content is worse than no fallback. It cannot be told apart from a legitimate state by anything downstream, and it silently accrues real progress against content the player does not own.

The phantom enemy party (fixed on DEV 2026-08-03 — not yet on release)

Reported by D: reloading landed on a level 70 enemy party — Garrote, Crestmantis and Kesh — with identical HP on every reload, on an account nowhere near that content.

This is the phantom starter again, on the other side of the field. initUnits() builds all six slots from one mock literal and then re-builds only the ally side: the if(RELEASE) block replaces a0, a1 and a2 and stops, so e0/e1/e2 shipped as the Designer-era seed — Garrote (Rare, Lv 70, hp 138/229), Crestmantis (Uncommon, Lv 70, hp 178/229) and Kesh (Assassin avatar, Lv 70, hp 196/200), four moves each. Those are literals, which is why the HP never varied.

It was not a resume. The battle snapshot in localStorage was assumed to be the source, and namespacing it per environment and account changed nothing. It reproduces with localStorage completely empty: the party arrives in the component's initial state (units:this.initUnits()) at mount, and setState never writes units once during boot. Every later floor rebuild (refreshPartyFromCsv) re-seeds it.

reenter() made it live rather than cosmetic. It is the one battle entry that calls initUnits() and never assigns e0/e1/e2, so it opened a real fight — phase:'select', round 1, four moves a side — against a party the character had never met. enterBattle() assigns all three through mkEnemy, and challengeBoss() assigns the boss plus its two actives, so both of those were always clean.

Fix: mkEmpty now takes a side, and the RELEASE block empties the enemy slots exactly the way it empties an unused ally slot — hp:0, ko:true, empty:1, which ORDER/aliveOrder() already skip. reenter() refuses a field with no living enemy and returns to the floor rather than fabricating a battle out of leftover state; the floor is where the server owns the pool.

The general shape, again: mock content surviving in a path that sanitises only half of what it builds. The ally half of this same function was fixed on 2026-08-02; the enemy half was never looked at.

Client: initUnits() / mkMon / mkEmpty, battleRewards(), reenter(), the onEqCurrentClick unequip branch in play/app.js. Probe: tools/probe_p1.py (5/5); repro: tools/repro_p1.py.

Changelog

  • 2026-08-03 — phantom ENEMY party removed (ON DEV; release still carries it until the next merge). initUnits() seeded a mock Lv 70 enemy party (Garrote/Crestmantis/Kesh) that the release sanitiser never stripped, and reenter() opened a real battle on it. Enemy slots now empty the same way ally slots do, and reenter() refuses an empty field. Root cause found by instrumenting the booted client, not by reading code: it reproduces with localStorage entirely empty and with zero setState writes of units. Verified 5/5.
  • 2026-08-02 — page created; phantom starter removed; party made swap-only. An empty creature slot yielded the hardcoded Scoria/Taproot starter, which fought and earned. It now yields an empty unit that cannot act and earns nothing, and the reward loop refuses it explicitly. Unequipping a party creature is refused outright, so the empty state cannot be created any more. Verified 10/10 against the reporting account's own live save.
