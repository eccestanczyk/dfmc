THE ACTIVE PARTY

Other Systems › The Active Party

Your team is your character and two creatures. You set it in the Equipment window: the two creature slots, Monster I and Monster II, take any creature from your soul bag, and a slotted creature can be swapped for another but never removed — the party is always full. Each party card shows the creature’s live stats and its innate aptitude chart, the same chart the picker shows.

Swap-only

  • A creature slot can be replaced by picking another creature from the soul bag. The outgoing creature returns to the bag with its own record: XP, grade, mutant status and origin all go with it.
  • A creature slot can never be emptied. Clicking the equipped creature to unequip it is refused with CAN’T UNEQUIP THE ACTIVE PARTY, ONLY REPLACE. Gear slots are unaffected and still unequip normally.
  • Each party creature wears up to two Tags (Tag A and Tag B). A tag’s level requirement is tested against that creature’s level — see Equipment.
  • Auto-optimize never changes the creature slots; only you do.
  • A save from before the swap-only rule may still hold an empty creature slot. It fights with nobody: it never takes a turn, cannot be targeted, earns no XP, and cannot lose you the battle while your character stands.

The aptitude chart on the party card

  • Both party cards draw the same aptitude chart the picker draws: the creature’s innate 0–9 aptitude in Attack, Defense, Utility and Speed, from its codex entry. You can compare a candidate against the creature it would replace shape for shape.
  • It sits beside the portrait and does not replace the stat numbers. Those are the live HP / ATK / DEF / SPD after level, tags and quality; the chart is the creature’s innate aptitude. Two different facts.
