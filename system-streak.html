WIN STREAK & FLOORS

Other Systems › Win Streak & Floors

The tower is 100 floors — 10 zones of 10 — and the Void Apex continues it to 120. Each floor is cleared by winning a streak of consecutive battles; a single loss resets the streak to zero. Difficulty pressure comes from that reset, not from resource attrition.

Clear condition

  • Win a streak of consecutive battles on a floor to open the portal to the next floor. Each floor sets its own number of wins: floors 1–100 follow ceil(floor^0.65) (1 win on floor 1, 5 on floor 9, 13 on floor 49, 20 on floor 99); the Apex grind floors take a flat 30.
  • Losing a battle resets the streak to zero. You are not removed from the floor — the team stays put and rebuilds. No forced exit, no floor lock, no return to floor-select.
  • Leaving a floor resets the streak to zero. The streak belongs to the floor you are standing on, not a running total you carry. Any change of floor clears it: ascending, descending, the death-descent and the floor selector alike. Only a move that lands you back on the floor you started on keeps it. The between-floors loading screen reminds you.
  • This is by design: stepping off a partly-cleared floor forfeits that progress, and it must be rebuilt from zero. A streak that banked across floors would let you collect wins on easy floors and spend them on hard ones.
  • A PvP fight never touches the streak, won or lost.
  • Above floor 100 the formula does not apply. In the Void Apex postgame zone (floors 101–120) every grind floor takes a flat 30 wins to open the boss room above it, and bosses sit on every even floor instead of every tenth.
  • Boss floors (10, 20 … 100, and every even Apex floor) have no streak — the floor is the boss room, one fight, entered with that zone's boss key (see Bosses).
  • Full HP is restored after every battle, win or lose. There are no healing items; attrition exists only within a single battle.

Streak counter

After every battle a compact rectangle shows X / Y (wins / threshold). On a win, X scrolls up and the text pops briefly; on a loss, the rectangle cracks and X rolls back down to 0. It only appears on floors not yet cleared, and it can be switched off under Settings › Battle & Autoplay › Streak Counter (on by default).

Autoplay behaviour

Auto-Advance Floors

When the threshold is met and the portal opens, advance automatically (on by default). Off → keep farming the current floor. Autoplay never auto-enters boss rooms.

Stop Advancing After a Loss

Off by default. On, a lost battle switches Auto-Advance Floors off until you turn it back on — the streak reset becomes a pause, not a slide.

On Death

Stay — remain on the floor and rebuild the streak. Go Down (default) — drop one floor and continue there. A boss loss never descends: the boss room is left, not the floor.

Wild creature levels scale with floor (floor ±3, bell-distributed), and floor equals level — so the streak threshold and the enemy levels both climb together as you ascend. Which advancement stage appears in the wild is set per line by each creature’s advancement level (see the Creature Codex), not by floor bands.
