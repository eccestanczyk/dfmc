WIN STREAK & FLOORS

Other Systems › Win Streak & Floors

The tower is 100 floors — 10 zones of 10 — and the Void Apex continues it to 120. Each floor is cleared by winning a streak of consecutive battles; a single loss resets the streak to zero. Difficulty pressure comes from that reset, not from resource attrition.

Clear condition

  • Win a streak of consecutive battles on a floor to open the portal to the next floor. The wins each floor takes are authored per floor (floors.csv, column Wins_To_Clear); floors 1–100 follow ceil(floor^0.65) (1 win on floor 1, 5 on floor 10, 13 on floor 50, 20 on floor 99), the Apex grind floors take a flat 30.
  • Losing a battle resets the streak to zero. You are not removed from the floor — the team stays put and rebuilds. No forced exit, no floor lock, no return to floor-select.
  • Leaving a floor resets the streak to zero — INTENDED (D ruling, reaffirmed 2026-08-03). The streak is a property of the floor you are standing on, not a running total you carry. Any change of floor clears it: ascending, descending, the death-descent, and the floor selector alike. The only case that keeps it is a move that lands you back on the floor you started on. In code the streak survives exactly when floorNum is unchanged (streak:(prev.floorNum!==target?0:prev.streak) in the travel path).
  • The consequence is deliberate: stepping off a partly-cleared new floor forfeits that progress and it must be rebuilt from zero. Reported as a bug (report #17, closed wontfix) — it is the design. The pressure in this game comes from the reset, and a streak that banked across floors would let a player accumulate wins on trivial floors and spend them on hard ones.
  • Above floor 100 the formula does not apply. In the Void Apex postgame zone (floors 101–120) every grind floor takes a flat 30 wins to open the boss room above it, and the odd/even alternation replaces the every-10th boss rule. LIVE on release since 0.48 (2026-08-13) — floors.csv authors 30 on every Apex grind floor and the client gates the boss room on it through winsToClear().
  • Boss floors (10, 20 … 100, and every even Apex floor) have no streak — the floor is the boss room, one fight, entered with that zone's boss key (see Bosses). Which floors are boss floors is authored in floors.csv (Is_Boss), not an “every tenth” rule.
  • Full HP is restored after every battle, win or lose. Healing items were removed entirely; attrition exists only within a single battle.

Streak counter

After every battle a compact rectangle shows X / Y (wins / threshold). On a win, X scrolls up and the text pops briefly; on a loss, the rectangle cracks and X rolls back down to 0. It only appears on floors not yet cleared, and it can be switched off under Settings › Battle & Autoplay › Streak Counter (on by default).

Autoplay behaviour

Auto-Advance Floors

When the threshold is met and the portal opens, advance automatically (on by default). Off → keep farming the current floor. Autoplay never auto-enters boss rooms.

Stop Advancing After a Loss

Off by default. On, a lost battle switches Auto-Advance Floors off until you turn it back on — the streak reset becomes a pause, not a slide.

On Death

Stay — remain on the floor and rebuild the streak. Go Down (default) — drop one floor and continue there. A boss loss never descends: the boss room is left, not the floor.

Implementation notes

  • The clear is the server’s count (server-authority run, 2026-10-22; on release since 0.89). Each credited win on an uncleared floor advances a per-floor count kept on the account, the clear is count ≥ Wins_To_Clear, and the victory screen adopts the server’s streak / cleared / justCleared when the answer lands. The game client predicts the same numbers from its own counter so the screen never waits.
  • The client’s counter resets to 0 on a loss and on a floor change. The server’s count is reset only by a credited win on another floor — a loss is never reported to it — so after a loss the next win shows the server’s figure again. The rule the game intends is the one in Clear condition; where the two disagree the server’s figure is the one that clears the floor.
  • Both sides read floors.csv; ceil(floor^0.65) is kept in code only as the fallback for a floor the sheet has not loaded.
