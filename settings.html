Hub › Settings

The in-game Settings menu. One full-screen panel with a section rail on the left and options on the right (mobile: sections collapse into an accordion). Every option persists locally and syncs with cloud save. Defaults are chosen for a first-time mobile player.

Layout

  • Opened from the gear icon on the main HUD. Full-screen overlay, dark parchment-and-iron chrome consistent with the codex aesthetic.
  • Section rail (left, Cinzel labels): Audio · Display · Battle & Autoplay · Controls · Adventure Log · Notifications · Account & Data · Language & Accessibility · Legal & Support.
  • Control types: slider (0–100), toggle, segmented (2–4 exclusive choices), dropdown, action button (opens dialog/page).
  • Footer bar: Restore Defaults (per open section, with confirm) and game version/build string.
  • Changes apply instantly — no Save button. Destructive actions always confirm.

Controls

Status: LIVE on release since 2026-09-02 (0.67). Keyboard only — a touch device has no keys to bind, and the section changes nothing there.

OptionControlValuesDefault
Toggle AutoplayKeyany single characterA
Open / Close EquipmentKeyany single characterE
Open / Close InventoryKeyany single characterI
Lock / Unlock HoveredKeyany single characterL

Click a row to arm it, then press the key. Clicking the armed row again, or Escape, cancels; Backspace or Delete unbinds (the row prints an em dash and matches no key). Space never binds. Closing Settings drops an armed row without binding anything. One key belongs to one action: binding a key another row already uses takes it from that row.

The rule. A bound key calls the same thing the on-screen button calls, so the keyboard can never reach a state a click cannot. A key is ignored while a text field has focus, while Ctrl / Cmd / Alt is held (browser shortcuts win), on auto-repeat, and at character creation. Inventory opens the item bag; with the soul bag already open it switches to the item bag rather than closing.

Lock / Unlock Hovered (SHIPPED IN 0.87, 2026-09-28): with the pointer over a creature or item tile in either bag, the key toggles that record’s lock — the same LOCK the detail panel and creature sheet toggle, ignored by every dismantle. It does nothing when the pointer is off the tiles, while a panel covers the bag, or in dismantle-select mode.

NEW badge (SHIPPED IN 0.87, 2026-09-28; a bag display rule stored here): a creature or item that arrives in a bag — a hatch, a drop, a craft — wears a red dot and NEW at the bottom left of its tile until the pointer passes over it (a tap on touch). Records moving between the bag, the equipment slots and the pens are not arrivals, and what a load brings is the baseline, never an arrival. The unseen set is a list of uids in this per-realm settings store (bag.fresh), pruned when a uid leaves; Restore Defaults does not touch it.

  • 2026-09-25 — Audio: the sliders read as loudness, the master is a bounded trim, and every clip shares one ceiling (#995, #993, D). SHIPPED IN 0.87. #995: “60% is loud and 30% is impossible to hear, seems muted. Go research how other games implement these sliders.” Measured (dfmc-client/tools/afx_measure.py, through the client’s own decoder) the clips behind the Sound Effects slider spanned 31 LU at the same slider position — an impact at −8 LUFS was “60% is loud”, a creature clip at −35 LUFS under a g0.4 was “30% is muted” — so the slider was reporting the clips, not a law. The clips are trimmed to one ceiling now (−18 LUFS, never above −1 dBFS; Audio Review and codex/AFX_SPEC.md rule 3 own it). The law itself, researched: Music and Sound Effects keep (pct/100)^1.6, which is Stevens’ power law — the slider is percent of full loudness: 50% is half as loud (−9.6 dB), 30% is −16.7 dB, 10% is −32 dB, 0 is silence — the curve that the square, cube and dB-linear sliders of other titles approximate, and the one that keeps 30% clearly audible. Master Volume leaves that curve: two loudness faders in series compounded (master 10 × effects 30 was −48.7 dB, silence), and the OS volume is the real master, so this one is a trim — 0 = mute, then dB-linear from −24 dB to 0 dB with a linear roll-off to silence under 10% (dr-lex, Programming Volume Controls). Master at 10 / 30 / 60 / 80 / 100 is now −21.6 / −16.8 / −9.6 / −4.8 / 0 dB (was −32 / −16.7 / −7.1 / −3.1 / 0); Sound Effects at 30 under any master of 10 or more never lands below −38.3 dB. UI Sounds is unchanged. No row, default or save shape changed. Gate tools/probe_00995.py 14/14, red 4/14.

  • 2026-09-24 — Audio: the five sliders do something. LIVE ON DEV since 2026-09-24 (0.85). The Audio section has been documented above since the settings spec and not one of its rows had ever been read — there was no audio engine in the client. Master, Music and Sound Effects are real buses now (master feeding music and sfx), tapered rather than linear so a slider does not spend half its travel above comfortable, and they take a new value while the finger is still on them. UI Sounds stays a multiplier on the interface cues specifically, on top of the effects bus, because it is a category of sound and not a bus of its own. One new row, Mute All Sound, cuts the master to zero and remembers where every slider was, so unmuting restores the mix instead of a default. The bindings ride the same per-realm settings store as every other option, so they follow cloud save and Restore Defaults like the rest; no save shape changed and no server call was added. Mute in Background is still documented and still does nothing — it needs a page-visibility hook and is not in this batch.
