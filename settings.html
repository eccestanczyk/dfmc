The in-game Settings menu: one full-screen panel with a section rail on the left and options on the right. Every option is kept on this device and saved with your climb.

Layout

  • Opened from GAME SETTINGS in the game menu. The cogwheel beside the AUTOPLAY button opens it on Battle & Autoplay.
  • Section rail (left): Audio · Display · Battle & Autoplay · Controls · Tutorials · Notices · Notifications · Account & Data · Language & Accessibility · Legal & Support.
  • Control types: slider (0–100), toggle, segmented (2–4 exclusive choices), key, action button (opens a dialog or page), and display-only rows.
  • Footer bar: Restore Defaults (the open section only, with a confirm) and the game version.
  • Changes apply instantly — no Save button. Destructive actions always confirm.

Controls

Keyboard only — a touch device has no keys to bind, and the section changes nothing there.

OptionControlValuesDefault
Toggle AutoplayKeyany single characterA
Open / Close EquipmentKeyany single characterE
Open / Close InventoryKeyany single characterI
Lock / Unlock HoveredKeyany single characterL

Click a row to arm it, then press the key. Clicking the armed row again, or Escape, cancels; Backspace or Delete unbinds (the row prints an em dash and matches no key). Space never binds. Closing Settings drops an armed row without binding anything. One key belongs to one action: binding a key another row already uses takes it from that row.

The rule. A bound key does the same thing the on-screen button does, so the keyboard can never reach a state a click cannot. A key is ignored while a text field has focus, while Ctrl / Cmd / Alt is held (browser shortcuts win), on auto-repeat, and at character creation. Inventory opens the item bag; with the soul bag already open it switches to the item bag rather than closing.

Lock / Unlock Hovered: with the pointer over a creature or item tile in either bag, the key toggles that record’s lock — the same LOCK the detail panel and creature sheet toggle, ignored by every dismantle. It does nothing when the pointer is off the tiles, while a panel covers the bag, or in dismantle-select mode.

NEW badge (a bag display rule kept with your settings): a creature or item that arrives in a bag — a hatch or a drop — wears a red dot and NEW at the bottom left of its tile until the pointer passes over it (a tap on touch). Records moving between the bag, the equipment slots and the pens are not arrivals, and what a load brings is never an arrival. Restore Defaults does not clear it.
